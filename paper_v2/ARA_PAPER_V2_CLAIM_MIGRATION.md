**Working title:** *Accumulation–Release Asymmetry: A Geometric Relational Framework*
**Prepared:** 4 August 2026
**Purpose:** bridge the April 2026 Zenodo preprint to the current framework without silently retaining, removing or strengthening a claim.

## Reading rule

Every material statement from the April paper must be assigned one of five dispositions:

- **Retained:** still used with substantially the same meaning.
- **Generalized:** survives as a narrower instance of a larger ARA rule.
- **Narrowed:** part survives, but the original scope was too broad.
- **Superseded:** later tests or definitions replaced the statement.
- **Open:** remains a physical or empirical hypothesis rather than a core result.

The Version 2 paper must distinguish definitions, exact mathematical consequences, typed crosswalks, empirical results and conditional physical hypotheses.

## Central proposal and crosswalk boundary

The paper should introduce the motivating physical proposal early:

> ARA proposes that the recursively coupled two-pole wave/sphere relation is a bedrock generative geometry from which larger physical identities and their observable relations emerge.

This is deliberately larger than the present evidence. It is a **conditional physical hypothesis**, not an exact consequence of normalizing a relation to `0–2`.

Because ultimate bedrock geometry cannot currently be observed directly, the research programme proceeds in stages:

1. **Exact crosswalk:** translate an established equation or conservation relation into declared ARA coordinates while preserving the original result.
2. **Structural recovery:** begin from predeclared ARA rules and recover independently known relational architecture without inserting the target answer after inspection.
3. **Empirical discrimination:** use the same rules to make a frozen prediction that can lose to non-ARA controls on untouched data.
4. **Cross-domain recurrence:** test whether independently defined identities recover the same structure without changing poles, boundaries or instruments to rescue failures.

The first stage establishes mathematical compatibility and a shared language. The second can show non-trivial compression or convergence. The third and fourth supply empirical evidence for or against the larger physical hypothesis. No single crosswalk proves universal generation.
