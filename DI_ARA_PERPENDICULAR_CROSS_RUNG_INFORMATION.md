# Di-ARA — the minimal perpendicular cross-rung information structure

**Recorded:** 3 August 2026  
**Originator and name:** Dylan La Franchi  
**Status:** canonical ARA framework definition with an exact two-coordinate
mathematical scaffold; universal physical instantiation remains a hypothesis

## Outcome first

**Di-ARA** names two ARA relations working perpendicularly as one coupled
parent coordinate.

A single ARA always reduces to a Phase-A/Phase-B relation on one declared
`0–2` axis. Di-ARA does not replace that binary basis. It is the smallest
coupling that retains a second, perpendicular relational distinction when an
identity hands information between rungs.

At coarse quadrant resolution, the two axes produce four mixed states:

\[
\boxed{Ab,\quad aB,\quad Ba,\quad bA}.
\]

They are not four fundamental substances or four independent TE-ARA energy
budgets. They are four relational addresses generated by two binary ARA cuts.
Every address remains recursively decomposable into its own Phase A and Phase
B. Greater complexity therefore adds branches, not a different bedrock
geometry.

Generic Di-ARA contains no Phi, `1/e`, octave-amplitude or irrationality
requirement. Those belong only to typed applications that use the Di-ARA
geometry.

## 1. Exact coordinate definition

Let the two declared ARA coordinates be

\[
x\in[0,2],
\qquad
y\in[0,2],
\]

with ridge-centred coordinates

\[
\xi=x-1,
\qquad
\eta=y-1.
\]

The Di-ARA state is the ordered pair

\[
\boxed{D=(x,y)}.
\]

Away from either ridge, the signs of \(\xi\) and \(\eta\) produce exactly four
open quadrants. The canonical ARA chart used in the quantum and bubble work is

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
Ba & Ab\\
\hline
bA & aB
\end{array}
\]

or, with the ridge axes shown conceptually,

```text
                 y > 1
          Ba       |       Ab
                   |
        ------------+------------  x = 1
                   |
          bA       |       aB
                 y < 1
```

The mixed notation retains two features at once:

- the letters retain the Phase-A/Phase-B composition;
- order and capitalization retain which mixed route is leading in the
  declared chart.

The capitalization is relational, not an intrinsic permanent property of an
object. Axis orientation and pole naming must be declared before measurement.

When `x=1` or `y=1`, the state lies on a Di-ARA ridge between adjacent sectors
and must not be forced into one of them. At `(1,1)` both projections are on
their ridges; activity, direction and child structure are required to tell a
null crossing from an active double-ridge state.

### 1.1 Exact reciprocal/log realization

When one declared ARA cut compares consecutive positive magnitudes, define

\[
s=\frac{M_{n+1}}{M_n},
\qquad
u=\log s,
\qquad
x=\frac{2s}{1+s}
=1+\tanh\left(\frac{u}{2}\right).
\]

This gives an exact translation between the framework and the mathematics:

| ARA language | Reciprocal/log mathematics |
|---|---|
| Phase-A side | `s<1`, equivalently `u<0` |
| ridge | `s=1`, equivalently `u=0`, hence `x=1` |
| Phase-B side | `s>1`, equivalently `u>0` |
| phase/anti-phase reversal | `s ↔ 1/s`, `u ↔ -u`, `x ↔ 2-x` |
| identity amplitude | reciprocal endpoints `1/alpha ↔ alpha` |

For \(\alpha>1\), the exact ARA endpoints are

\[
x_A=\frac{2}{1+\alpha},
\qquad
x_B=\frac{2\alpha}{1+\alpha},
\qquad
x_A+x_B=2.
\]

Thus \(\alpha\) is not a universal ARA constant. It is the measured amplitude
of one identity in one declared observable and rung. The invariant structure
is reciprocal opposition and reflection around the ridge.

The second Di-ARA cut may be another reciprocal ratio or a separately
normalized signed relation. In the first Irrationality Di-ARA implementation,
it is signed phase traversal:

\[
y=1+\frac{\delta}{\pi}.
\]

The signs of `log s` and `delta` then generate the four `Ba/Ab/bA/aB`
sectors. The radial pair alone is one ARA; crossing it with the signed phase
pair is the Di-ARA.

### 1.2 Conditional child-to-parent composition

If a physical parent ratio is generated multiplicatively from child ratios,

\[
s_P=c\prod_i s_i^{w_i},
\]

then its signed log-asymmetry is exactly

\[
u_P=\log c+\sum_iw_i\log s_i,
\]

and its reciprocal amplitude is

\[
\alpha_P=e^{|u_P|}.
\]

This formalizes the ARA statement that the parent's identity is the surviving
asymmetry of its children plus their coupling. Equal opposite contributions
can leave a parent ridge while remaining visible when the children are
decompressed. Unequal contributions create a non-ridge parent amplitude.

The algebra is exact conditional on the multiplicative composition. Whether
a physical identity uses that composition, and how `c` and the weights are
measured, requires a frozen child-to-parent test. Di-ARA does not make the
composition true merely by naming four sectors.

## 2. The quadrant cycle and flips

One oriented walk around the declared chart is

\[
\boxed{
Ab\rightarrow aB\rightarrow bA\rightarrow Ba\rightarrow Ab.
}
\]

The exact chart reflections are

\[
F_x(x,y)=(2-x,y),
\qquad
F_y(x,y)=(x,2-y),
\]

and the full diagonal reflection is

\[
F_{xy}=F_x\circ F_y=F_y\circ F_x.
\]

Therefore

\[
F_x^2=F_y^2=F_{xy}^2=I.
\]

On the canonical labels:

- `F_x`: `Ba ↔ Ab` and `bA ↔ aB`;
- `F_y`: `Ba ↔ bA` and `Ab ↔ aB`;
- `F_xy`: `Ba ↔ aB` and `Ab ↔ bA`.

This distinguishes a one-axis handover from a full diagonal flip. A domain
test must determine which transformation occurred; it may not select a
different flip for each event after seeing the answer.

## 3. Why Di-ARA is the minimal cross-rung store

Suppose a coarse cross-rung record must preserve two independent binary
distinctions—for example, the side of the ridge on one ARA cut and the side of
the ridge on a perpendicular cut. There are

\[
2\times2=4
\]

possible joint states. Any lossless discrete label must therefore have at
least four addresses. Di-ARA supplies exactly four.

This is the precise bounded meaning of the framework statement:

> Di-ARA is the simplest way to store information across rungs.

A single scalar ARA cut can place the state on one gradient, but two different
histories can share that same projection. The perpendicular cut retains one
additional orientation/relation needed to distinguish the four mixed paths.
At coarse quadrant resolution this is equivalent to two binary labels. It is
not a claim that every physical Di-ARA literally stores two Shannon bits;
noise, inaccessible channels and boundary choice can reduce recoverable
information.

## 4. Fractal decompression and branching

The four Di-ARA states are mixed branches, not terminal objects. Each can be
promoted to a new measurement boundary and decomposed again:

\[
Ab\longrightarrow(Ab)_A\leftrightarrow(Ab)_B,
\]

and similarly for `aB`, `Ba` and `bA`.

For a fully expressed binary recursion, descending one generation doubles the
number of branch addresses. Adding one independent ARA axis also doubles the
coarse address space. Thus:

\[
\text{one ARA axis}\rightarrow2\text{ sides},
\qquad
\text{Di-ARA}\rightarrow2^2=4\text{ mixed sectors}.
\]

The physical system need not occupy every mathematically available branch.
Missing, inaccessible or weak branches must remain missing rather than being
filled from TE-ARA bookkeeping alone.

## 5. TE-ARA accounting

Each constituent ARA axis has its own normalized complete account:

\[
\mathrm{TE\!-!ARA}_x=2,
\qquad
\mathrm{TE\!-!ARA}_y=2.
\]

This does **not** mean the Di-ARA parent has a scalar TE-ARA of `4`. The two
totals refer to perpendicular declared accounts. Once coupled and viewed as
one parent identity, that parent is normalized at its own tier:

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{TE\!-!ARA}_{D}=2}.
\]

Decompression exposes the two child accounts beneath it; compression reads
one complete parent. This is the same perspective rule used elsewhere in ARA.

## 6. Relation to Information³

Information³ begins with two identities and their retained relation. Di-ARA
is the simplest perpendicular state chart capable of carrying that relation
across a rung without compressing everything into one scalar projection.

The two ideas are related but not identical:

- Information³ states what relational content must be retained;
- Di-ARA supplies a minimal four-sector address for two perpendicular binary
  distinctions;
- a specific reconstruction law must still show how a parent and its retained
  relation recover the children.

Therefore Di-ARA is a candidate geometric carrier of Information³, not an
automatic proof that any four-quadrant dataset is information-preserving.

Their use as a universal physical cross-rung path is not established. T333

## 9. Test requirements

A physical Di-ARA claim must predeclare:

1. the parent identity and rung;
2. two independently meaningful ARA observables;
3. their pole orientations, ridges and units;
4. why the cuts are perpendicular or independently informative;
5. the chart-to-label map for `Ab`, `aB`, `Ba`, `bA`;
6. boundary handling at `x=1` or `y=1`;
7. the proposed adjacent or diagonal flip;
8. intact-lineage, broken-lineage, order and projection controls;
9. evaluation and untouched holdout rules.

Four occupied quadrants alone establish only the coordinate classification.
A storage claim additionally requires reconstruction or held-out prediction;
a physical flip claim requires the frozen transformation to beat alternatives.
