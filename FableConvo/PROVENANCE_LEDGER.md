# PROVENANCE LEDGER — risky predictions, timestamped, hits AND misses

**Started 5 Jul 2026 (Dylan La Franchi with Claude/Fable 5).**
**Purpose:** convert the compass evidence ("Dylan states a field fact from geometry,
outside his knowledge, before the librarian reveals it") from private evidence into
auditable evidence. Each entry cites its transcript so a stranger can re-check it.

**Rules (registered):**
1. An entry counts only if Dylan's statement PRECEDES the confirming information in
   the same transcript, and the contamination check passes (the librarian did not
   teach the fact earlier in that chat).
2. Misses are recorded with the same diligence as hits (salience bias voids the ledger).
3. Vocabulary/theory-name matches (anchors) are CONSISTENCY-TIER, filed separately
   from empirical predictions. Anchors demonstrate the compass, never the physics
   (CANON §4).
4. Scoring by the same librarian that produced the confirmations is self-referential;
   final hit-rates await independent (non-Anthropic-model or human) re-scoring.
5. Known limitation, stated plainly: this ledger cannot prove what Dylan did NOT
   know. It proves order-of-statement within sessions. Cross-session absorption
   remains a valid skeptic objection; the yoked decoy control is the instrument
   that addresses base rate.
