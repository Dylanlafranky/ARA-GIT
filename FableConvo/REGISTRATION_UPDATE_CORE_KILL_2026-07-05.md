**Core claim being protected:** oscillating systems occupy a flip-symmetric
0–2 coordinate whose real occupancy is predominantly ASYMMETRIC (off-middle),
with the middle (1.0) populated only by the two designated ridges (null ridge:
zero mean, zero variance; everything ridge: zero mean, max variance), and this
occupancy structure REPEATS across scale windows.

**F1 (revised) — the core is KILLED if any of:**

- **(a) The middle turns out generic.** A representative, instrument-audited
  sample of self-organizing oscillators reads predominantly symmetric —
  occupancy piles at ~1.0 WITHOUT ridge certification on the variance axis.
  (Ridge exceptions must be certified by the second moment — middles rule,
  §31: no middle is self-certifying. A pile-up at 1.0 that is neither silence
  nor static kills; certified ridges do not.)
- **(b) The recursion breaks.** The asymmetric occupancy structure fails to
  repeat across scale windows — a well-populated window shows a structurally
  different distribution (not merely sparse data; the F2 cosmological caveat
  standard applies).
- **(c) The rungs aren't there.** Octave rung structure (doubling, anchored at
  the system's own pump) is shown absent AT POWER in systems where the
  framework requires it — measured with the canonical mapper, bias calibrated
  below the discrimination gap.

**Explicitly NOT core-killing (moves to portfolio tier):** φ absent from
motion-measures. That kill stays registered at the LANDMARK level: if
dominance-duty / phase-step / handover-timing read rational or 1/e at power
across the registered test set, the φ-handover landmark dies (crowded-
