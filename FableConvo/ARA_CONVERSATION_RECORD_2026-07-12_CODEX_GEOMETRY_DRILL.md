\[
\underbrace{Q_{net}}_{\text{Gauss source balance}}

-
\underbrace{|Q_-|}_{\text{negative entries}}.
\]

Likewise, Gauss cannot distinguish “no enclosed charge” from “large equal positive and negative enclosed charges.”

sphere area by four. The signed total flux remains \(q/\varepsilon_0\). Gauss therefore preserves the enclosed source
