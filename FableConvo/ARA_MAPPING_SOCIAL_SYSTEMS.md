**Status: MAPPING (correspondence exercise).** Framework vocabulary paired with

## 1. Economy

**The rung ladder (named, pre-existing):** Kitchin ~3-5 y (inventories),
Juglar ~7-11 y (credit/investment), Kuznets ~15-25 y (construction),
Kondratiev ~45-60 y (technology; CONTESTED — possibly artifact, fence hard).
Adjacent-rung ratios ~2.2-2.6 across the ladder.
**Octave anchor (the strongest):** Sornette's discrete scale invariance —
log-periodic precursor oscillations in bubbles with preferred scaling ratio
lambda ~ 2. The fractal has a favorite rung spacing, measured, near two.
**The asymmetry as central object:** Minsky cycle — slow leverage
accumulation, fast release ("stairs up, elevator down"; return-distribution
skew is measured fact). The framework's founding quantity is post-Keynesian
macro's founding story.
**The ridge split, high fidelity:** efficient market = EVERYTHING RIDGE
(zero-mean unpredictable returns, maximal variance — the fair-lottery
mapping already in the repo). Crisis = LOCK EVENT: pairwise correlations
spike toward 1, diversity dies, the market moves as one body (measured
daily in risk management). Normal->crisis = everything-ridge -> lock-pole
transit.
**Candidate motion-measures (unregistered):** dominance duty between credit
band and inventory band; phase-step of leading indicators across cycles.

## 2. Society

**The field exists:** cliodynamics (Turchin) — demographic-structural theory:
elite overproduction and popular immiseration ACCUMULATE, instability
RELEASES. Secular cycles (~200-300 y) + a ~50 y instability rhythm in the
US record. Data infrastructure: Seshat databank.
**Pre-registered hit on record:** 2010 prediction of instability peaking
~2020. (Their registration, not ours — cited as precedent that
accumulation-release forecasting in social systems can be done with
falsifiable form.)
**Framework reading (interpretive):** institutional trust as the slow band,
political mobilization as the fast band; the handover measures are
unmapped territory.

## 3. Language

**Dylan's pointing ("grammar dependent vs subject dependent waves") lands on
closed-class vs open-class:** function words (grammar) = few, short,
top-of-Zipf, CLOSED set; content words (subject) = many, longer, open set,
constantly growing. Grammar = lattice; content = flow.
**The arrow (the best anchor in this doc):** GRAMMATICALIZATION —
content words cool into function words over centuries (verb -> auxiliary ->
affix), and the process is famously near-unidirectional. Information
crystallizes into structure and almost never melts back. A measured
time-arrow in every documented language family: flow -> lattice, never
lattice -> flow. The framework's cooling story in the historical record.
**The speech-signal rung ladder (acoustic, gate-safe):** phoneme (~tens of
ms) -> syllable (~100-300 ms; universal narrow band ~4-8 Hz modulation
peak across languages) -> phrase (~seconds) -> discourse. Roughly doubling
spacing.
**Scale-free signature:** Zipf's law (rank-frequency ~ 1/rank);
Menzerath-Altmann (larger wholes, smaller parts) as cross-scale coupling law.
**Candidate motion-measures (unregistered):** duty of the syllable-band
envelope vs phrase-band envelope in recorded speech corpora; S-curve
(logistic) handover timing in historical language change (adoption of new
forms — well-documented, data exists).

## 4. What this mapping does and does not claim

DOES: the ladder, the asymmetry, the ridge split, the octave-ish spacing,
and the cooling arrow all have named, measured instances in these fields —
the coordinate reads these territories without distortion.
DOES NOT: none of this discriminates generator from classifier (every
anchor is the host field's own result). The phi motion-claims are wholly
untested here; candidate measures listed above are UNREGISTERED. Reflexivity
makes several physics instruments unreliable in these domains — any future
registration must state how it survives Goodhart.
