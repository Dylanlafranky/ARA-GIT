# What is ARA — for people

**Dylan La Franchi · consolidated 2 July 2026 (with Claude/Fable 5).** One page of what,
one of why, one of status. The evidence trail lives in the git; this is the map legend.

## The idea (one paragraph)

Ordinary coordinates tell you where an object is. ARA tries to tell you where a
**relationship** is. Take any two-sided, reversible relation — accumulate/release,
compress/expand, form/erode, store/discharge — and ask: where between the two poles does
the system sit, which way is it moving, at what scale, coupled to what, carrying what
history? ARA is the attempt to give those questions one shared coordinate system.
The idea is extremely simple. The evidentiary trail is not.

## The geometry (current model)

- **The 0-2 folded scale.** Two poles and a middle. The poles are the two deaths:
  **lock** (all holding, nothing moves) and **dissipation** (all release, nothing kept).
  Flip-symmetric: 0<->2 is a labelling choice; what is physical is distance from the
  middle and direction of travel. Convention, declared once: up = slower/larger,
  down = faster/smaller; every document states its orientation at first use.
- **The middle (1.0) is two different places** that need the variance axis to separate:
  the **null ridge** (perfect cancellation: zero mean, zero variance - silence) and the
  **everything ridge** (all waves at random phase: zero mean, MAX variance - static/the
  bath). A fair lottery lives at the everything ridge. Both average to nothing; only
  one is quiet.
- **Octave rungs (the space ruler).** Structure recurs by doubling, per system, anchored
  at that system's own pump. Spacing laws are PER-SYSTEM claims; the cross-system atlas
  is an orientation map, not evidence for any base.
- **phi, in two states (the time ruler - hypothesis under test).** The same constant has
  a rest-form and a motion-form. At rest: the pentagon (proportions golden, rotations
  rational - phi webbed shut, closing in five). In motion: the golden angle
  (137.5 degrees - the handover that never closes). The framework's registered claim is
  that phi appears ONLY in caught-in-motion relational measures (band-dominance duty,
  phase-step per cycle, handover timing) and is ABSENT from slice/shape measures. That
  boundary is pinned; if the motion measures also read rational, there is no retreat left.
- **The meta-axis (circle vs line).** Every real system is a spiral between pure
  oscillation (e^(i*theta), the circle - all memory, no transfer) and pure decay
  (e^z, the line - all transfer, no memory). Its angle between them is measurable
  (the damping ratio / Q). Where oscillation ceases (critical damping), ARA's
  jurisdiction ends. e is the constant of no-geometry and is kept OUTSIDE the framework
  as its null hypothesis, permanently.
- **The full state** is more than the scalar: (position, orientation, rung, phase, path,
  coupling, energy, variance, occupancy). The scalar is a compressed address.

## The relations (how things interact)

Impact travels through connections with a fixed asymmetry: same-rung and down-rung
neighbours feel it strongly; up-rung feels only the average (the slaving principle).
The present belongs to the biggest wave; the future belongs to the slower structure
underneath; what cannot be modelled from inside a slice appears as memory plus noise
(and the noise enters by the same door the shed leaves through - one coupling, two-way).
Relations carry information the parts do not have (synergy), and the bond is paid for
out of the pair (1+1 = 2 - bond in content; more than 2 in what the pair can reveal).

## The why

Every field's deepest dimensionless number turned out to be a local instance of ARA's
template (1/Q in atoms, the Rouse number in rivers, duty in oscillators, flux ratios in
cascades) - which is guaranteed by dimensional analysis and is exactly why the
coordinate works everywhere as a CLASSIFIER. Whether it is also a generator - whether
phi genuinely governs the handovers - is the open bet, decided by the test queue, not
by this document.
