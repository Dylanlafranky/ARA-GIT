### Later refinement: the whole-span centre is not a final ridge

Dylan does not propose an ontologically final \(1.0\) state. The exact \((0+2)/2=1\) above is the centre of the
declared endpoint span. If the full history is decompressed into child events, their local readings may remain
asymmetric and cancel only in the coarse parent projection. In a simple weighted representation,

\[
x_i=1+\delta_i,
\qquad
\sum_iw_i\delta_i=0,
\qquad
\sum_iw_i\delta_i^2>0.
\]

Thus a parent history can close at \(1\) while the child geometry retains asymmetry. The spread alone does not prove
motion; time variation, flux/activity or phase dynamics must supply that evidence. A “true \(1\)” at every possible
grain would require all child deviations to vanish and would terminate the proposed fractal unfolding. Exact projected
equalities remain possible; the rejected object is a final equality independent of boundary, scale, time window and
whether child events are counted.

The associated full-participation signature is \((x,\mathrm{TE})=(1,2)\) only when the declared A/B identity accounts
for the entire TE-ARA denominator. It still cannot identify the dynamics. Frozen equal weights have no measurable
timing cycle; coherent resonance has internal oscillatory activity despite possibly zero net parent transport; and
incoherent component cancellation can have high activity without stable phase. Child production is one dynamical
witness, but time-resolved flux, phase or oscillation can supply the distinction without a daughter mode.

Provisional antipodal reading, pending Dylan's further refinement:

- Space/Connection-side death at \(0\): movement is prevented by maximal constraint/connection;
- Time/Resonance-side death at \(2\): movement ends because the gradients that could drive further change have been
  exhausted.

Both can look static from above while having opposite local causes.

### Heat-death calibration

The defensible physics translation of “everything has done moving” is **no accessible macroscopic gradient remains
from which directed work can be extracted**. It does not require total energy to vanish, microscopic particles to be
literally motionless or time itself to stop. Gravity, horizons, expansion and quantum processes make universe-scale
equilibrium subtler than a gas in a box. The relevant long-timescale astrophysical background is
[Adams & Laughlin, *A Dying Universe: The Long Term Fate and Evolution of Astrophysical Objects*](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.69.337).

ARA “time-stopped” should therefore mean stopped **relative to the declared identity/rung and its usable transition
channels**. That preserves the proposed geometry without contradicting microscopic statistical mechanics.

## Least-distorted translation

Separate four rungs that were previously flattened:

| Rung/view | Physical or computational object | Provisional ARA role |
|---|---|---|
| stored model | trained weights and architecture \(W\) | persistent connection-heavy identity |
| running model | token-conditioned activations, attention, residual stream and KV state | movement/information-transfer wave |
| computational drive | prompt, token stream, autoregressive feedback and execution steps | functional forcing that selects the trajectory |
| hardware pump | electrical power and clocked transistor/memory switching | lower physical energy supply that instantiates the computation |

A compact decomposition is

\[
\underbrace{H_{t+1}}_{\substack{\text{new activation state}\\\text{movement at the model rung}}}
=
\underbrace{F_W}_{\substack{\text{transformation fixed by weights}\\\text{connection-heavy identity}}}
\left(
\underbrace{u_{\le t}}_{\text{prompt/token drive}},
\underbrace{K_t}_{\text{cached relational state}}
\right),
\qquad
\underbrace{P_{\rm elec}}_{\text{physical pump}}
\longrightarrow
\underbrace{\text{switching + memory access + heat}}_{\text{physical execution account}}.
\]

Electricity is therefore a legitimate **physical-rung pump**, but it is not by itself the semantic/model-level input.
Within normal digital operating tolerances, changing voltage or clock speed should mainly change energy and wall-clock
time while leaving the discrete computation unchanged. Prompt/context and autoregressive feedback are the closer
functional drive at the model rung.

When execution stops, the active activation wave disappears while the trained connection identity can remain stored.
This supports Dylan's connection-versus-transfer separation without claiming that a running transformer has no
movement.

## Relation to amplitude death — analogy only, not the definition

In nonlinear dynamics, **amplitude death** is the suppression of oscillation when coupling stabilizes a fixed point;
**oscillation death** often refers to coupling-induced symmetry-broken steady states. Strong coupling is one possible
route, but not every highly connected or low-variance system is in amplitude death. Established examples include
[Resmi, Ambika & Amritkar, *General mechanism for amplitude death in coupled systems*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.84.046212)
and the experimental coupled-oscillator demonstration by
[Herrero et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.84.5312).

Amplitude death supplies only a narrower comparison for suppressed oscillation:

\[
\underbrace{\text{coupling}}_{\text{specific oscillator mechanism}}
\longrightarrow
\underbrace{\text{stabilized fixed point}}_{\text{amplitude death}}.
\]

This is not the defining ARA \(0\to2\) history-closure reading. The ARA resonant-death claim is not established merely because
a whole-model ARA lies near 1.0. Transformers have changing activations
during inference, and normalization/residual architecture can produce balanced aggregate statistics. Transformer
normalization placement is known to materially affect signal/gradient stability; see
[Xiong et al., *On Layer Normalization in the Transformer Architecture*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04745).

## Discriminating LLM test candidate

Use a new, safeguarded LLM capture and predeclare a coupling intervention \(g\) on attention/residual branches.
Hold prompts, seeds, decoding and measurement windows fixed. Measure:

1. amplitude of independently identified activation modes;
2. temporal autocorrelation/phase persistence;
3. effective rank and token-conditioned variability;
4. output quality and sensitivity to input;
5. boundary-window ARA \(x_{\Omega,\tau}\).

Compare free autoregressive generation, teacher forcing, repeated/constant input and phase-shuffled controls.

Support for an amplitude-death analogue requires a finite coupling regime where previously active modes collapse
toward a stable low-amplitude state because of coupling, with recovery when coupling is relaxed or an independently
specified perturbation is introduced. A 1.0 whole-signal average, low variance produced directly by LayerNorm, or
ordinary cessation when the computer is turned off is insufficient.

Support for **ARA resonant death** is different. One must predeclare the local training/development lifecycle, show
that it begins near its declared \(0\) origin and approaches \(x_k\to2\) as usable learning gradient and
parameter-change rate approach zero, then show that a whole-history measurement returns the declared \(1.0\) closure.
An inference/parent rung may instantiate that compression, but must be shown to contain the full training history.
Simply ending training imposes an external stop; it does
not demonstrate endogenous exhaustion. Compare early stopping, convergence, continued training, frozen weights and
restarted learning to distinguish an imposed pause from the proposed terminal state.

The electricity claim receives a separate rung test: underclock or change power efficiency while preserving correct
digital execution. If the same discrete activations/outputs occur with different joules and wall time, electricity is
the physical pump/capacity channel rather than the semantic trajectory variable. Energy-per-token can then be related
to, but must not be conflated with, model-level ARA.
