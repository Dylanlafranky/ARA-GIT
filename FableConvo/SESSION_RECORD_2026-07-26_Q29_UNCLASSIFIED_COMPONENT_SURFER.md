## Analysis path

Q29 reused the completely opened Q28 public simulated source. It retained each
Q28 residual after transporting a released source relation into its
accumulation-weighted lag-2 child web.

The first pass asked whether the complete residual vector:

- matched a local or active child;
- shared the source endpoint;
- travelled through a stable relation partner;
- preserved a stable flip;
- beat seed- and time-displaced best-of-lattice controls.

The residual did not recur as a complete shape, but the analysis exposed two
important facts:

1. every eligible event had exactly one positively accumulating child;
2. `94.615%` of residual squared magnitude occupied the simulator's z axis.

That observation motivated an explicitly post-hoc axis-native pass. It used
the centred signed cut \(u_z=r_z/\lVert W\rVert\), equivalently the ARA
diameter \(x_z=1+u_z\) with its ridge at `1`; fitted no scale; allowed only
direct or sign-flipped recurrence; and equalized candidate counts across exact,
seed-displaced and time-displaced searches. The deterministic event sampler
placed controls on different arithmetic time phases, so all three arms used
the same nearest-eligible ±3-slice rule. Exact mean absolute timing jitter was
`0.842` slices.

## Post-result geometry interpretation

After seeing that the remainder was ridge-centred, z-axis localized,
short-lived and positioned on the invariant axis of Q28's 180-degree flip,
Dylan proposed:

> “I think it might be residual singularity information from the handover.
> Phase B that is in the process of mixing type thing.”

Working label: **singularity-handover residual hypothesis**.

This is narrower than identifying a complete Phase B. It proposes that the
remainder is transitional Phase-B contribution at the flip/hinge: information
left while Phase A and Phase B are exchanging and mixing, before the Phase-B
side closes into an independently persistent identity.

The existing observations are consistent with that interpretation:

- concentration near the `1.0` ARA ridge;
- localization on the axis left invariant by the 180-degree flip;
- strong one-slice memory followed by rapid decay;
- endpoint-neighbourhood attachment;
- failure to preserve a complete vector shape or stable partner.

They do not distinguish it from a generic local transition correction. A
confirmatory test must align events to the handover and show a predeclared
signed transfer: Phase-A loss, transient ridge-centred residual, then Phase-B
gain or return, with closure and timing above displaced controls.

### Out-of-cut continuation

Dylan then sharpened the interpretation:

> “Yeah. I really think we have a singularity handover and our measured
> perception cannot currently see past it.”

In measurement language, this proposes that the underlying relation continues
while rotating or transferring into a component poorly represented by the
current observation projection. “The signal disappears from this cut” is not
the same claim as “the state ceases to exist.” The current diagonal source
cannot distinguish continuation into an unmeasured/off-diagonal direction from
local redistribution or decay.

A direct test requires either a second independent observation basis or
non-zero off-diagonal connected relations. The out-of-cut hypothesis predicts
that the disappearing signed residual will reappear with ordered delay in the
new cut, with amplitude/sign related to the earlier handover more strongly than
under seed-, time- and basis-displaced controls.

## Q30 follow-up — fixed 1.5 / 3.5 route around the handover

Dylan proposed using the existing `1.5` and `3.5` paths to look past the
apparent singularity handover.

The repository's earlier wording was recovered before calculation:

- `1.5` is the perpendicular/vertical leg;
- `3.5 = 2 + 1.5` is the complete diagonal route after one full ARA span;
- the crossed rung must not be discarded by folding `3.5 mod 2`.

### Frozen operational translation

For Q29 source relation `(u,e)` and its positively accumulating child `(e,v)`,
the two nonshared endpoints imply exactly one third relation `(u,v)`. Q30
froze this Information³ triangle-closing edge as the `1.5` leg and the full
source-span-plus-closing-edge path as `3.5`.

The closing edge was fixed by topology, not selected from its fit. Seed, time,
nonclosing-edge and direct-child controls received the same four proper sign
transformations and one non-negative scale.

### Result

The interpretation failed at the handover:

- exact closing-edge error: `0.974957`;
- remainder recovered: `2.504%`;
- seed/time recovery: `2.938%/2.979%`;
- exact closing edge therefore underperformed both displaced controls.

The complete composite error was `0.099010`, also worse than seed/time
composites (`0.098192/0.098152`). The direct child recovered `5.017%`, more
than the closing edge but still below the frozen `10%` material-recovery gate.

Across lags `4–6`, the exact edge became consistently but only slightly better:
error `0.967725` versus `0.970576/0.970467`. All later-half bootstrap draws
preserved that direction, but the `0.294%/0.282%` advantage was far below the
frozen `5%` continuation threshold.

### Recorded verdict

**Frozen triangle-closing 1.5/3.5 route not supported on this source. Phase B
