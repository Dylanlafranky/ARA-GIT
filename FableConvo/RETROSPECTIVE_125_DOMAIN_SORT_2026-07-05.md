## Honest verdict

**The negative half of 125's map holds cleanly.** Every post-April φ-absence
in the gravity and nuclear domains (galactic spacing, rotation retraction,
fission rationality) landed where 125 said absence would land. Several
entries currently filed as φ *misses* in the provenance ledger are, under the
125 map, *conforming outcomes* — the misses ledger and the domain map are
consistent, not in tension. Absence predictions are also the
least-contaminated kind: nobody wishfully measures a null into existence.

**The positive half is NOT established.** In the domains where 125 promises
φ, the record shows: two stellar misses (Cepheid, gradient direction), two
crowded-neighborhood consistent-withs that cannot discriminate (sunspots,
QBO), one weak-form survivor (club-lean), and April-era biological numbers
that predate the canonical mapper and calibration rules. The intermediate
class tolerates the ENSO/PDO duty nulls but predicts nothing sharp.

**Net:** 125 survives as a half-confirmed domain map — the φ-jurisdiction
boundary is holding from the OUTSIDE (where φ shouldn't be, it isn't), while
the inside (where φ should be) remains undischarged by the crowded
neighborhood. This sharpens the registered claim to:

> φ, if it does work anywhere, works only in caught-in-motion relational
> measures OF feedback-coupled systems — two pre-registered fences,
> intersected (motion/slice boundary × 125 domain map).
