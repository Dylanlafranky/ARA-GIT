## 4. Axiomatic consolidation

The framework was re-read against the axiomatic, historical files, physics
crosswalks and previous prime/quantum work. The following statements were
clarified and retained.

### 4.1 ARA, TE-ARA and octave are one geometry

ARA is the `0–2` diameter/cut through a two-pole wave or sphere. It is both a

\[

TE-ARA is also not different geometry. It is a bookkeeping label introduced

### 4.2 Phase budget

Phase A and Phase B are each represented locally by `1` at their equal
meeting. Each opposing wave can traverse the full `0–2` span, so their
uncoupled maximum capacities would sum to four. Because they are in direct
opposition inside the selected identity, only a total local budget of two is

\[
\mathrm{TE\!-!ARA}=A+B+O=2.
\]

`O` is not arbitrary error. It is declared unresolved, external, cross-rung or
fractal participation. In pure two-phase bookkeeping it is zero; real systems
may expose gravity, friction, detector response, neighbouring relations or
unresolved children through it.

### 4.3 Poles, ridge and recursion

- `0` and `2` are singularity poles and wave-spawning endpoints.
- `1` is the equal-and-opposite ridge for the chosen identity and cut.
- Which pole is called connection/space or traversal/time may be mirrored, but
  the direction must be declared.

  the next scale or a perpendicular cut.
- A normal completed child contributes about `0.5` at the direct parent rung.
- A child sitting at its own `x=2` resonance pole can fill an entire parent
  phase allowance rather than the ordinary half projection.

The ridge may mean cancellation, equal opposition, maximum flow, densest core
or a transition boundary depending on the identity. Those are different
physical expressions of the same relational location, not automatic synonyms
in every dataset.

### 4.4 Mixed-state notation

The four common mixtures remain

\[
Ab,\quad aB,\quad Ba,\quad bA.
\]

The leading capital/lowercase role records identity ownership or direction;
capitalisation records which component is stronger. This casing is not
decorative because `Ab` and `bA` can have different directional meaning even
when their unsigned ingredients are similar.

### 4.5 Information³

For two information-bearing identities, the minimum relational account is

\[
A,\qquad B,\qquad R_{AB}.

The relation is itself information. Adding a new information packet creates
new relations rather than merely appending one isolated scalar. Geometrically,

Information³ therefore has two linked readings:

1. the minimum formation of a relational web;
2. the recursive expansion of that web as identities and relations are added.

ARA is a compressed diameter reading of this relation. Di-ARA is two ARA
identities coupled often enough to form a common parent. ARA⁹ is the next

## 5. Phi demotion and the Irrationality Di-ARA

### 5.1 The hypothesis that was tested

The working idea had been that `1/e ↔ e` described a straight/radial axis and
`1/phi ↔ phi` described a curved/circumferential axis, with their interaction
forming an irrationality Di-ARA. The motivation was not numerology: coherent
non-closure appeared necessary to preserve asymmetry and information without
falling into perfect resonance or unstructured randomness.

step across qutrit, bubbles and river holdouts. None of the three primary
holdouts selected `e` radially, the golden step angularly, or the joint pair.

T341 tested whether observations near the pure line and circle axes converged
to those constants through one shared budget. The universal package again
failed. Qutrit retained an `e`-like radial lead, but a calibration-fitted value
was closer; the proposed coupled `e/Phi` gradient was not recovered.

The conclusion was deliberately narrow:

- the radial/angular or line/circle decomposition remained useful;
- exact `e`, `1/e`, `phi` and reciprocal-Phi were not universal poles;
- a nearby `3/8` result was not to be renamed Phi;
- Phi became a possible identity-specific landmark or route, not Di-ARA
  itself and not a universal free-running law.

### 5.3 What survived

Irrationality/non-closure still had a clear potential role: preserving
ordered movement and future information without exact low-order closure. The
framework therefore shifted from “find Phi everywhere” to “measure the nature
of non-closure, determinacy, address reuse and history.”

The general Di-ARA remained two coupled ARA axes with four mixed regions. The
Irrationality Di-ARA is one use of that geometry, not the definition of
Di-ARA itself.
