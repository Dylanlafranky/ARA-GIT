## 6. The budget — identity and relation share a conserved allowance (the named law)

The two knobs of §3 are not independent, and the law binding them has a name: the
**Englert–Greenberger–Yasin duality relation**,

**V² + D² ≤ 1**

- **V** = fringe visibility — the measured strength of the third term, the swing from dark
  to bright. This IS |γ₁₂|: the relation's aliveness, read off the screen.
- **D** = which-path distinguishability — how much *identity* information exists about which
  slit the quantum took. Not whether anyone looks: whether the information is *anywhere*.

The inequality is a hard budget. Every bit of "which thing is it" (identity) is paid for by
exactly that much loss of "how do they trade" (relation), and the two can never sum past the
allowance. Partial identity → partial fringes, quantitatively, confirmed in the lab.

In framework terms this is the sharpest statement quantum mechanics makes on our behalf:
**identity and relation are not merely different readings — they are conserved rivals.**
The connection column and the information column of the four-pole grid, bound by an
inequality at the bottom of physics. A system fully known as a *thing* (D=1) has no readable
relations (V=0 — the pole, the flat screen); a system fully alive as a *relation* (V=1) has
no thing-identity at all (D=0 — pure coupling, no fact of which path). Everything real sits
inside the budget, part thing, part trade.

## 7. The eraser — the ridge↔pole toggle is reversible (information, not disturbance)

The quantum eraser closes the case that this is about information rather than clumsiness.
Tag the paths (D→1): fringes vanish — the pole. Then **erase** the tag downstream — destroy
the which-path record before comparing — and the fringes **return** (in the correlated
subsets). Nothing was un-disturbed; no particle was touched twice. The relation revived
because the identity-information ceased to exist, and the budget swung back.

So the decohered pole is not damage — it is a *bookkeeping state*, and the ridge↔pole toggle
runs both ways. Which is the framework's reading stated by the apparatus itself: the third
term lives or dies by what information exists about identity, because relation and identity
are the two spendings of one allowance (§6).

## 8. Tie-off — where this sits in the framework, and the fence restated

- The **circle** (§1) is the literal phasor; time drives the rotation (e^(−iEt/ℏ)); only
  relative phase is observable — relation readable, absolutes not.
- The **third term** (§2) is the relation as a number — pattern without quantity (§4), the
  aboutness ledger's entry, integrating to zero while carrying all the structure. "The swing
  is the information" is exact: decoherence is a mean-preserving killing of the swing — the
  second moment dies, the first survives. (The null-ridge/everything-ridge distinction, in
  its original habitat.)
- The **budget** (§6) is identity↔relation as conserved rivals — the connection/information
  opposition, bounded by theorem.
- The **eraser** (§7) shows the whole structure is informational, reversible, and about what
  exists rather than what is watched.
