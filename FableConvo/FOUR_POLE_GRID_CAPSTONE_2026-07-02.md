## 2. The connection column — shared structure that cannot signal

Everything the session placed here is real, named, and mute:

- **The constraint sector of gauge fields.** Every gauge field splits into a radiative part
  and a constraint part. The Coulomb field around a charge and the Newtonian field around a
  mass are the constraint parts: bound to sources, unable to carry a message. In fiber-bundle
  language these fields ARE mathematical objects called **connections** — Dylan's word is the
  technical term.
- **The elsewhere.** At every event, information rides the null cone — a measure-zero sliver
  of directions (light, "the line") — while the spacelike bulk ("the elsewhere") is causally
  mute yet gravitationally woven. Connection without information, filling almost all angles.
- **Dark matter's phenomenology.** Couples through gravity (connection) only; no radiative
  channel. Confirmed structural consequence: no channel → no cooling → no collapse into
  disks → puffy spherical halos. The disk-inside-halo shape of every galaxy is the visible
  portrait of the two columns.
- **Entanglement** (placement contested — see §6). Maximal shared structure, zero signaling.

**The cell's mathematical identity (anchor):** Shannon's two ledgers come apart completely.
*Mutual information* I(X;Y) — how much two things share, static — is a different quantity from
*channel capacity* C — how much can be sent. An entangled pair realizes the extreme case:
**I maximal, C = 0.** "Connection is static information" is that inequality in words.

## 4. The coupling between the columns — conversion economics (theorems)

The columns are convertible but mutually exclusive in use — Dylan's "anti-phase," formalized:

- **Teleportation arithmetic:** moving 1 qubit costs exactly **1 ebit (connection, consumed)
  + 2 classical bits (information, light-speed).** Neither resource alone moves anything.
  Connection × information = transport, with a fixed exchange rate.
- **Resource theory of entanglement:** static resources (ebits) are converted into dynamic
  tasks (qubits moved) under local operations + classical communication. You hold the
  connection or you cash it — never both in the same act.
- **Information-gain / disturbance trade-offs:** extracting moving information from a system
  degrades its static correlation. Using the connection spends it.
- **Monogamy of entanglement:** the correlation budget is finite; deep connection to one
  partner precludes deep connection to others (also why "wild" pairs decohere: environmental
  entanglement eats the budget).

## 7. The walls (theorems — build with them, never against them)

1. **No-communication:** entanglement alone carries zero usable information; acting on one
   side changes nothing observable on the other, regardless of hidden geometry.
2. **No-cloning:** unknown quantum states cannot be copied. Historical note kept for the
   record: this theorem was FORCED into existence (Wootters–Zurek/Dieks, 1982) by refuting
   Herbert's FLASH — a 1981 device of exactly the session's proposed class ("manipulate one
   side of a pair to steer the other"). The cancel-the-anti-wave teleporter is refuted by the
   same walls — dead by theorem, the honorable death, in distinguished company.
3. **Monogamy:** the entanglement budget is finite; environmental coupling consumes it (why
   wild pairs are scrambled, why labs isolate, why local detection of entanglement is
   impossible — a lone member of a pair is indistinguishable from noise).
