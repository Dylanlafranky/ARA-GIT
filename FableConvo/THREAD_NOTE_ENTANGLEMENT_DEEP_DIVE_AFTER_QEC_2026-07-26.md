## Musing added after Q33B — relation across the singularity boundary

**Recorded:** 26 July 2026  
**Evidence tier:** musing / mechanism hypothesis; not a Q33B result

Dylan's current picture is:

> Entanglement may be the connection of lattice points across a singularity.
> An irrational or non-closing relation wave may travel around the unobserved
> side and return upward, joining two points on the boundary. From our cut it
> appears to blink out, but the relation has not ceased; it has stopped making
> rational/local relations with the surrounding measured lattice.

### ARA translation

- two visible endpoints sit on the measured boundary;
- their maintaining relation crosses outside the locally rational/closing
  lattice cut;
- the out-of-cut relation remains part of the complete TE-ARA account;
- its return preserves a nonlocal joint identity even when neither local
  endpoint contains the whole relation;
- apparent disappearance would therefore mean loss of local representability,
  not literal destruction of the relation.

### Established-physics comparison, without identification

This picture is compatible in spirit with the fact that entanglement is stored
in a joint state and cannot generally be reconstructed from either local
marginal alone. It is not yet a derivation of entanglement, a signalling
mechanism, a hidden trajectory or evidence that quantum amplitudes literally
travel around a geometric singularity.

### Future discriminating test

Use raw paired measurements to compare:

1. a local-only model built from both endpoint marginals;
2. the same model plus the frozen ARA boundary relation;
3. the exact quantum joint prediction.

Freeze the proposed out-of-cut route using exposed settings, then ask whether
it predicts untouched joint settings or decoherence/revival timing better than
the local-only account. No-signalling must remain an explicit gate: the
relation may organize joint outcomes without carrying a controllable local
message.
