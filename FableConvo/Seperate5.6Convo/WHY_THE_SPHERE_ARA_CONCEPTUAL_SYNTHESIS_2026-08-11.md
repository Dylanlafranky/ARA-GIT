## Abstract

*Why the Circle?* argued that the circle is an unusually natural candidate for ARA's planar expression of continuous relational closure. It can move continuously without losing closure, has no privileged handover point, survives arbitrary rescaling, becomes locally line-like at sufficiently shallow curvature, changes appearance under projection without necessarily changing identity, and admits exact phase-space crosswalks in ordinary mathematics and physics.

The sphere is not proposed here as a second primitive.

It is the directional decompression of the same object.

If the circle is closure with no privileged handover point in a plane, the sphere is closure with no privileged orientation. An oriented ARA diameter is one cut through that larger identity. Rotating the diameter changes the relation being measured while leaving the proposed parent identity intact.

This paper adds a second route to the sphere: the **Fractal Path**.

A broad parent identity can be progressively decompressed into children, children of children, and relations which themselves become identities at the next resolution. Each descendant can remain locally complete while its direct projection onto a distant parent becomes progressively weaker. The information does not disappear. Rung distance, lineage, orientation, and the path connecting descendant to parent are themselves relational information.

In a discrete teaching picture this produces a branching tree. In three dimensions, however, the branches need not point only left or right. They can differentiate through every available orientation. As directional resolution becomes increasingly fine, the discrete branches can approach a continuously resolved frontier. The sphere is therefore proposed as the completed geometric view of what the Fractal Path looks like when relational differentiation is resolved in every direction.

The central compression is:

> **The tree is the discrete developmental view. The sphere is the continuously resolved view.**

This gives the sphere an information interpretation.

- The **core** is the compressed common parent.
- Moving outward represents increasing relational differentiation / decompression.
- A **branch** is one Fractal Path through progressively finer children.
- Radial or rung distance records how far the current identity has been decompressed from the selected parent.
- Angular separation can represent divergence between relational paths.
- The **surface** is not where information becomes simple. It is where increasingly fine distinctions become collectively smooth.
- The boundary is not necessarily an absolute wall. It is an interface at which the identity's next relations become important.

Earth provides an unusually intuitive physical teaching model. From far away it compresses into a simple approximately spherical parent. Moving inward in informational resolution reveals atmosphere, climate, ecosystems, geology, organisms, cells, molecules, and further children. Conversely, moving outward from a chosen Earth-centred parent, progressively finer directional distinctions can be imagined as branches approaching a smooth spherical frontier. Near the crust and atmosphere, individual children can be almost inconceivably small relative to the Earth parent while remaining collectively decisive for the parent's interaction with the next environment.

Earth does **not** prove ARA. Conventional gravity explains why massive bodies become approximately spherical under suitable conditions. The value of Earth here is narrower: it physically demonstrates how an extraordinarily simple spherical parent description can coexist with staggering recursively differentiated internal structure and a highly active relational boundary.

The stronger ARA hypothesis remains open:

> **A pure identity is a recursively decomposable sphere/wave closure, and what appears as discrete identity, connection, line, branch, surface, or trajectory depends on boundary, orientation, scale, and rung.**

# 1. Why the sphere is the next question after the circle

The circle paper asked:

> **What geometry can remain the same object while continuously moving, changing scale, changing measurement orientation, becoming locally line-like, and remaining a complete child inside a larger complete parent?**

The circle answered this unusually cleanly in a plane.

But ARA is not intended to be restricted to one planar cut.

The next question is:

> **What does the same closure look like when orientation itself is decompressed?**

The immediate answer is the sphere.

For a circle:

\[
x^2+y^2=R^2.
\]

For a sphere surface:

\[
x^2+y^2+z^2=R^2.
\]

The important ARA move is not merely adding \(z\).

It is removing the privileged plane.

A circle has no privileged handover point along its boundary.

A sphere has no privileged orientation over its surface.

Thus the earlier compression:

> **A circle is closure with no privileged handover point.**

naturally extends to:

> **A sphere is closure with no privileged orientation.**

The sphere is therefore not a rival shape to the circle.

It is the same closure resolved across more directions.

# 2. Mathematical terminology: sphere, surface, and ball

Standard mathematics distinguishes:

- the **2-sphere** \(S^2\): the surface satisfying
  \[
  x^2+y^2+z^2=R^2;
  \]
- the **3-ball** \(B^3\): the volume satisfying
  \[
  x^2+y^2+z^2\le R^2.
  \]

ARA language has often used “sphere” more broadly for the whole relational identity, including the idea of interior structure and multiple cuts.

That is acceptable internally if the distinction is remembered.

For public technical writing:

\[
\boxed{
\text{sphere surface }S^2
\neq
\text{ball/interior }B^3.
}
\]

In this paper:

- **surface** refers to the mathematical spherical frontier;
- **interior** refers to the radial/decompression structure inside that frontier;
- **ARA sphere identity** refers to the proposed whole relational closure, for which the exact mathematical model may require both surface and interior information.

This distinction matters because the Fractal Path picture is explicitly about structure **from a compressed core outward to a continuously resolved frontier**.

A child that appears tiny from the parent rung can contain an enormous internally resolved world when entered locally.

This is the same intuition previously used in the fruit-fly / human / Earth extrusion example.

# 9. Why the triangle appears

At the first cut:

\[
A\xleftrightarrow{R_{AB}}B
\]

is naturally line-like.

But once:

\[
R_{AB}
\]

is treated as an informational identity, there are three informational identities:

\[
A,\quad B,\quad R_{AB}.

The minimum straight-sided closed 2D polygon has three non-collinear vertices.

Therefore the triangle is an unusually clean geometric model of Information³:

```text
              R_AB
             /    \
            /      \
           A ------ B
```

The triangle is not claimed to prove that all information is literally Euclidean.

It is the smallest geometric closure in which the two identities and their relation can all be represented as informational vertices inside one locally closed object.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{
\text{ARA line / relational cut}
\rightarrow
\text{Information}^3\text{ triangle / local lock}.
}
\]

The triangle is the decompressed lock of the relation already implicit in the line.

# 12. The tree is still too flat

A botanical tree is a useful teaching projection because it shows:

- parent;
- children;
- branching;
- ancestry;
- divergence.

But it is too flat for the full ARA object.

It tends to imply:

- one privileged trunk direction;
- edges that do not themselves contain structure;
- fixed node/edge roles;
- branches that occupy a predefined space;
- and a tree whose global hierarchy is already known.

ARA requires something richer.

The tree is **discovered while being traversed**.

A branch can later be found to share a previously unseen parent with another branch.

An edge can become an identity.

A child can become a parent.

A parent can be demoted to a child inside a larger closure.

Thus the Fractal Path is better described as:

\[
\boxed{
\text{recursive weighted relational decompression}.
}
\]

# 25. Earth does not prove ARA

The Earth analogy must not be promoted beyond what it supports.

Earth's approximate spherical shape has an established physical explanation.

For a sufficiently massive self-gravitating body, gravity tends to pull matter toward the centre of mass. Under appropriate conditions, hydrostatic equilibrium produces an approximately spherical body, modified by:

- rotation;
- internal density differences;
- tectonics;
- topography;
- atmosphere;
- tides;
- and other interactions.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Earth is spherical}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{ARA sphere ontology is proven}.
}
\]

The useful evidence is narrower:

\[
\boxed{
\text{a simple spherical parent can physically coexist with enormous nested relational structure}.
}
\]

Earth also demonstrates that a smooth parent boundary can be an emergent/coarse description of extraordinarily fine lower-scale children.

That is highly compatible with the ARA picture.

Compatibility is not proof.
