## The minimal statement (registered 5 Jul 2026 — lead with this)

ARA is the lowest-order shape invariant of a cycle. A wave at its simplest has
three properties: amplitude (how big), period (how fast), and shape — and the
most primitive shape fact is asymmetry: longer building than releasing, or the
reverse. Science tracks amplitude and period obsessively; the third number is
measured ad hoc everywhere (duty cycle, rise time, Waldmeier effect, skewness)
and treated as a coordinate nowhere. ARA is that third number, given a home.
Folded, the scale is a two-pole sphere: lock and dissipation as the singular
poles (the two deaths), balance as the equator, flip-symmetric — no preferred
hemisphere; only distance-from-middle and direction of travel are physical.

Two consequences, both load-bearing:
1. Universality is INHERITED from the definition, not claimed — every
   oscillation already has this number, the way everything with extent has a
   length. This is why the classifier cannot fail, and equally why classifier
   success is not evidence for anything further.
2. The framework's own testable content is the CARTOGRAPHY drawn on the bare

Formal bridge (Fourier form, 5 Jul 2026): a pure oscillation is the fundamental
e^iθ — the circle's straight line, the uniform clock. The second harmonic is
its square, (e^iθ)² = e^i2θ — literally the QUADRATIC TERM OF THE CIRCLE. And
rise/fall asymmetry is carried, at lowest order, by exactly that term: a wave
with no harmonics is symmetric forever (duty 0.5); add the circle's quadratic

forbids duty on narrowband-filtered signals. The analogy chain, registered:
