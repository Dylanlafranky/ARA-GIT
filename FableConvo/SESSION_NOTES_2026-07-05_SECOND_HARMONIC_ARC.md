## 4. The two shears (ANCHOR + registered-claim linkage)

Key mathematical fact: a pure circle seen through ANY linear/oblique/sheared
projection remains a single clean sinusoid — coordinate shear alone cannot
produce a lean (no second harmonic appears). Therefore:

- **Axis shear (space-side):** changes the projection WINDOW, not the shape.
  This is the registered 36° claim: a length-2 axis seen through 36° shows
  2·cos 36° = φ — ¶ "we miss the 0-2 and only get 0-phi." Confirmed by Dylan
  this session: "You are right about the 0-phi being the shear."
- **Time shear (phase-flow):** the lean requires nonuniform traversal — the
  phase running fast through one half of the cycle and slow through the
  other. The shear ARA measures is a SHEAR OF TIME AROUND THE LOOP
  (accumulate slow, release fast = the clock running at different rates on
  the two halves). This is the formal content of "The Geometry of Time."

Two shears, two observables: space-shear sets what fraction of the axis is
visible (0–φ); time-shear sets the lean of what is visible (duty).
