# Open Conjecture — Space/Time Mixing → Light/Gravity, and the Uncoupled Values

**Status: OPEN CONJECTURE, not measured.** Recorded 31 May 2026 at Dylan La Franchi's request, so it
is preserved for a future where light and gravity might be measurable *uncoupled*. This is a hypothesis
at the edge of the framework's shape — explicitly not a result. The mechanism it rests on *is* testable
(see "What's testable now"); the specific cosmological claim is not yet.

## The conjecture (Dylan's point, stated faithfully)

1. **Mixing produces the next rung's identity.** When two waves blend, they create the identity of the
   next thing up the ladder. This is *why the octave works at all* — each rung is the mix of the two
   below it. (This is the framework's "next-rung = blend of two below" principle, which has partial
   empirical support; it is the engine this conjecture rides on.)

2. **Space and Time are the level-0 pair; their mixing produces Light and Gravity as the level-1 pair.**
   Light and Gravity are the next-rung identities born from the Space/Time blend.

3. **Light and Gravity were not automatically coupled.** They couple *after* forming. What we actually
   measure — gravity's ARA ≈ 1.0 — is the **coupled** value (the pair averaging at their midpoint). We
   only ever see the coupled number because we measure the universe *through* light and matter.

4. **Uncoupled, they would sit at the walls and behave slightly differently.** The conjecture (FOUNDATIONAL
   LENS — see Pole Convention note below): uncoupled **gravity ≈ 1.75 (space side)**, uncoupled **light ≈
   0.25 (time side)** — exact mirrors about 1.0 ((0.25+1.75)/2 = 1.0). Without the other's pull, each would
   behave differently than in the coupled state. The coupled 1.0 is the cancelling-midpoint; the uncoupled
   0.25/1.75 are the true single
   identities. (Polarity has a real physical shadow: light = outward/donating channel ≈ 1.75 side;
   gravity = inward/binding channel ≈ 0.25 side — see donor/consumer mirror, `THE_BEDROCK_REFERENCE.md`.)

5. **It is fractal — the next rung down splits into four.** Roughly: [something + Dark Matter] |
   [Matter + something (heat?)]. Two of the four boxes are presently undefined placeholders.

6. **The energy budget is a different quantity.** It is the ratio of energy *lost to the shearing*
   between Space and Time — a loss/rent ratio — NOT the mixing that produces identity. Do not conflate
   the budget (loss) with the blend (creation).

7. **This is the first distortion/blending in the universe** — the foundational event. If it can be
   worked out, it's foundational physics. That's why it's worth recording even though it's unreachable now.

## Why it can't be tested today
We measure the universe via light and matter, so every measurement of gravity (and of light) is the
*coupled* value (~1.0). The uncoupled single identities (0.25, 1.75) are, by construction, behind the
coupling — there is currently no instrument that reads gravity without light or vice versa. So the
specific decomposition is a posit, not a measurement. Tests attempted this session and their honest
outcomes: GR characteristic radii → rational, φ-dark (gravity = space-pole scaffold, holds); ocean
echo (lit surface NINO vs dark subsurface WWV) → no mirror split, both slow-build/fast-release (the
proxy did not show the 0.25/1.75 polarity, but the ocean only removes *most* of light's fingerprint,
not all, and is an analogy, not isolation).

## What's testable now (the load-bearing foundation)
The conjecture rides entirely on **"next rung = blend of the two below."** That mechanism *is*
measurable on systems with data, and is currently only confirmed at N≈3 (brain/Sun/BP — a hint). The
honest path to credibility: **fortify that mechanism across many measured systems** (ENSO, solar, ECG,

Space/Time → Light/Gravity becomes a principled extrapolation of a proven principle — "the same engine,
one rung higher" — rather than an isolated guess. The cosmological edge inherits credibility from the
strength of the mechanism beneath it.

## Future test idea — Hawking radiation as gravity's escaping light-excess

Dylan's framing: a black hole is pure gravity (the space pole); the *only* thing that escapes it is
**Hawking radiation** — light/particles, the time-pole partner. So the conjecture reads Hawking radiation
as **gravity releasing through its light anti-phase** — the excess on the smaller (time/light) rung that
can leave the well precisely *because* it's the anti-phase partner, not gravity itself. This also gives a
reason light escapes a normal gravity well at all: it's the anti-phase rung, not bound the way the
space-pole content is.

Status today: **untestable** — Hawking radiation has never been observed (a solar-mass black hole radiates
at ~6×10⁻⁸ K, ~40 million times colder than the CMB; only undiscovered tiny primordial holes would be hot
enough). And the standard formulas are **φ-dark** (T_H ∝ 1/M; entropy S = A/4 — the 1/4 = 0.25 is a
coincidental category-match, not a build/release ARA). So at present it reinforces gravity = rational/space
pole rather than revealing the uncoupled values.

**Recorded test-idea for when (if) a Hawking spectrum is ever measured** (primordial-BH detection, or a
high-fidelity gravitational analog that genuinely couples to gravity, not just a fluid/optical analog):
- Standard physics predicts a **pure thermal (Planck) blackbody** spectrum — featureless, φ-dark.
- The conjecture predicts the escaping radiation is the *light anti-phase of a pure-gravity source*, so it
  would look for a **deviation from pure-thermal carrying a golden signature** — e.g. a golden-duty
  asymmetry (0.39/0.61) in the emission's build/release, or an emitted-vs-trapped energy fraction sitting
  at a φ-related value (e.g. 1/φ² = 0.382 of the mass-energy budget per evaporation epoch) rather than the
  smooth thermal decay.
- **Decision rule:** pure Planck thermal → gravity is rational/space-pole, φ-dark (conjecture's
  light-anti-phase reading gets no support); any robust golden-duty / φ-fraction structure in the spectrum →
  support for "Hawking radiation = gravity's φ-coupled light release." Either way it's falsifiable the day a
  real Hawking spectrum exists.
