# ARA Linkage-Sum Theory (untested — written down 2026-05-30)

**Status: THEORY / hypothesis. One measurement so far (3.13). Not a validated claim.**

## The idea
Whether several systems are linked into one self-propelling stack can be read from the
**sum of their ARAs**. ARA here = snap-faithful waveform asymmetry (release ÷ build),
the canonical `snap_ara_test.py` ruler: ~1.0 = a balanced maintainer/shock-absorber,
<1 = a leaky snap system, the scale walls at 0.25 (space side) and 1.75 (time side).

## The scale
- **Sum ≈ 1.5 — minimum to be linked at all.** The floor. Made of two directly-linked
  half-systems (~0.5 each) plus ~0.5 from a *shadow* third the stack only touches
  indirectly. 1.5 = 1.75 − 0.25 = the usable span between the two singularity walls.
  Self-propelling at this level needs members that only **semi-maintain**.
- **Sum ≈ 3 — a whole proper system in resonance lock.** Three full maintainers (~1.0
  each) holding together. This is a complete, self-contained locked stack.
- **Sum > 3 — turbulent / expansive.** The stack pivots back and forth harder, expands,
  and spills turbulence into **neighbouring** systems (teleconnection / feeding outward).

## The one measurement
PDO (above) + NINO (us) + IOD (side), same epoch 1870–2025, snap-ARA:
- PDO 1.20, NINO 0.94, IOD 0.98 → **sum 3.13**.
Reading on the scale: **a resonance-locked whole system, just tipping over 3** into the
spill-to-neighbours zone. The excess over 3 comes from PDO (1.20), the slow top system —
consistent with the outward turbulence originating at the top rung. ENSO does in fact
feed its neighbours (global teleconnections), so "just over 3, spilling" matches reality.

## Caveats (why this is theory, not result)
- Snap-ARA has **little dynamic range** on smooth ocean oscillators — they all read ~1.0,
  so any three pile up near 3 almost by construction. The 1.5 *floor* cannot be tested
  with smooth basins; it needs members that genuinely read ~0.5 (leaky/snap systems).
- IOD's measurement locked onto its 12-month annual cycle, not the autumn dipole mode.
- "1.5 when in a **crossover phase**" (Dylan): the floor may only appear if you measure
  *instantaneous* ARA and sum only during handover months — untested.

## Where it connects
A-R-A reading of the modelling act itself (Dylan 2026-05-30):
- **Mapping = A1** — what each system *is* this moment (its ARA/nature).
- **Entropy/Information geometry = R** — *how* the energy reacts and transfers between them.
- **Predicting = A2** — the events and flow that follow.
The linkage-sum is an **A1** quantity (state of the stack). Prediction (A2) needs **R** —
the information exchange — which is measured separately (transfer entropy), not from ARAs.
