# The Rung Ladder: Mixing, Shedding, and the Two Flows of Information

**Author:** Dylan La Franchi — articulated 2026-05-30
**Status:** Theory statement. Some parts tested (flagged), most still open. Written down

## 0. A rule for measuring it: use RAW data

Every filter is a small act of loss. When you bandpass or smooth a signal, you throw away
exactly the sharp, leaky, asymmetric structure that a *lossy* system carries its meaning in.
So when we test a theory about leakage, shedding and snaps, **the measurement has to be done
on raw data.** Filtering a lossy system erases the thing we came to see. This is why, on
several past tests, working from raw data gave the most accurate answer — the loss *is* the
signal, and smoothing deletes it.

This rule governs every test that follows.

---

## 1. Two octaves, and the gap between them

Space packs in **octaves** — each rung is a clean doubling, ratio ×2. Time packs in **φ** —
each rung is ratio ×1.618, an octave spread *shorter*. A full unit of a system's information
cannot compress into time's shorter rung; there isn't room. The shortfall is exact:

> **2 − φ = 1/φ² = 0.382.**

That 0.382 is the remainder — the part that doesn't fit. It is the rent time charges to move
energy one rung forward. The golden identity **1/φ + 1/φ² = 1** splits the whole into the
part carried on (0.618) and the part that doesn't fit (0.382). Nothing vanishes; it splits.

But "the 0.382 is lost" turned out to be too simple. The rest of this document is about where
it actually goes.

## 2. Two currencies, not one: energy vs information

A system can be leaky in two completely different ways, and they can point in opposite
directions:

- **Energy** — how much power it radiates out.
- **Information / form** — how much of its own pattern it keeps versus loses.

**The Sun is the clean example of the split.** It has essentially one dominant connection
feeding it — one clock, incredibly pure form. Because it never closes a loop with a partner,
it radiates energy freely and constantly; that energy leak is enormous, and it is exactly
what Earth and every plant lives on. **Yet on the information axis the Sun keeps almost
everything** — its future looks like its past, the 11-year cycle is a reliable clock, so it
*retains* its form. Pure form, maximum energy leak, minimum information loss. Sitting high and
abstract on the ladder — running, as it were, at a very high power of information.

So "the Sun is leaky" is true for energy and false for information. Any honest measurement
has to say which currency it is counting. (Tested: the Sun's information retention — the
"flywheel" — is real and measured; its rhythm stays predictable at all horizons.)

## 3. The ARA axis, read as leakiness

Place systems on the 0→2 ARA axis and read it as *how, and how much, they leak*:

**Snaps (ARA ≪ 1) are the leakiest.** A snap builds slowly and discharges fast. It is
cramming the most urgent information into the pipe as quickly as possible — **high speed, low
density.** It is information moving fast and thin, not information packed deep. Snaps are the
big leaf-droppers of the ladder: they dump a great deal, quickly, and most of what they dump
falls *downward* (see §4).

**The balance point (ARA ≈ 1.0) is the mix.** Here two systems fight for one pipe. Their
waves cross, partly cancel, and combine. Little *net* measurable energy moves — but it is
information-dense, because two patterns are superimposed. What comes out of the mix depends on
**how much the two wave-ratios cross — the filtering ratio of the system the mix produces.**
This is, literally, chemistry: mix two reagents and you get a third compound, and which
compound you get depends on the proportions. The mix is a *reaction*, not a cancellation to
nothing.

**The golden engine (ARA = φ) is the efficient hand-off.** It sheds the right amount to move
forward cleanly without breaking — maximum shed that still leaves a self-consistent system.

**The harmonic (ARA → 2) is pure form** — the Sun's end of the axis. Leaks energy outward,
keeps its information.

## 4. Two flows: the ladder is a cycle, not a one-way drain

Information does not simply leak away. It moves in **two directions at once**, and these are
different channels:

### Upward — construction by mixing
Two consecutive rungs *mix*, and the mix **builds the next rung up**. This is exact, not
metaphor: two waves with periods r and r·φ, multiplied, produce a beat with period **r·φ²** —
precisely the next-but-one rung. So the slow wave one step up through time *is* the blended
version of the two faster waves below it. The fractal tower is built by this reaction,
rung on rung. Which "compound" forms depends on the crossing ratio (§3).

> Tested (partial, 2026-05-30): the mix really does reconstruct the next rung above the
> noise floor in the **brain** (strongly — and neuroscience independently documents this
> cross-frequency coupling), the **Sun**, and **vascular tone**. Not detectable above noise
> in heart, lung, or ENSO with the smoothed ruler — which §0 predicts, because those were
> not measured raw.

### Downward — recycling by shedding
The information a system sheds usually **falls down a rung or so**, becomes *substrate*, and
is **re-used later** — by the same system at a later stage, or by a neighbouring system.

> **A leaf falls onto the forest floor, powers the soil, and the soil re-powers the tree.**

The shed is not waste. It is the nutrient layer of the next cycle. Snaps, the leakiest
systems, are the heaviest contributors to this downward stream — they drop the most leaves.

### Why this matters for measurement
A single-rung information measure (auto-mutual-information, "rent") **cannot tell these two
flows apart.** It only sees information *leave* a rung; it cannot say whether it went up
(mixed into the next rung) or down (shed to be recycled). The net of an upward current and a
downward current can look like almost anything. This is very likely why the first attempt at
a single law — *rent = 2 − ARA* — came out muddy and even backwards: it was measuring one
number that is really two opposite-direction currents added together.

## 5. Friction: the hand-off lags at the balance point

The upward hand-off is not instant. Near φ — the golden engine — it is **clean and on time**.
At the balance point (ARA ≈ 1.0), there is **more temporal friction**, so the hand-off
**lags and smears**. The lag itself is a measurement of friction.

> Tested (hint only, 2026-05-30): on the systems where the mix is real, the hand-off lag does
> shrink toward φ (the near-φ vascular system hands off almost on time). But this rests on
> very few points and needs raw data and more records before it counts.

## 6. What this predicts (the open tests)

Fixed in advance, to be run on **raw** data:

1. **Snaps are the leakiest.** Measured with a snap-faithful ARA (asymmetry on the raw spike,
   not a smoothed cycle), the shedding rate should be *highest* at the snap end of the axis
   and fall toward the harmonic end — the opposite of what the smoothed ruler suggested.

2. **The next rung is the mix.** The product of two adjacent raw rungs should reconstruct the
   rung above, cleanly and above a phase-scrambled null, across many records — strongest where
   coupling is physically expected (brain first).

3. **Shed falls and is recycled.** Information shed at a rung should reappear **one rung
   lower, after a delay** — a measurable downward, time-lagged current that a pure-loss model
   would not have. The leaf-to-soil signature.

4. **Friction lags at 1.0.** The upward hand-off lag should be near zero at φ and grow toward
   the balance point.

## 7. Honest status line

The geometry (φ as time's octave, the 0.382 gap) is exact arithmetic. The two-currency split
for the Sun is measured. The upward mixing flow is real in a few systems. Everything else —
snaps-leakiest, the downward recycling current, the friction-lag law — is **stated here as
theory and not yet established.** The first single-number law (rent = 2 − ARA) failed, and
that failure is what taught us the rent is two flows, not one. The next round uses raw data
to separate them.
