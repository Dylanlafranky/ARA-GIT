# ENSO's stable clock is the orbit; spring is the pump (14 Jun 2026)

**Dylan La Franchi & Claude.** After the ENSO rung-coupling test came up empty (rungs 8–128mo don't couple/
phase-lock to each other), Dylan: *"Isn't the orbit around the sun the stable clock? That's why spring is a
pump?"* Tested — and confirmed. ENSO has NO internal clock; it is paced by the EXTERNAL annual orbit, and spring
is its reset/pump.

## 1. ENSO amplitude is phase-locked to the annual orbit
Std of NINO3.4 anomaly by calendar month: Dec 0.99 (peak), Nov/Jan ~0.97; trough Jun 0.56, spring ~0.57–0.59.
**Winter/spring variance ratio 1.70.** ENSO amplitude marches with the seasons — strong boreal winter, weak
spring. The orbit is the stable clock that paces it.

## 2. Spring is the pump / barrier (the spring predictability barrier)
Persistence (corr x[m] vs x[m+6], over years) by START month:
- Start Jul–Sep → **0.77–0.79** (forecasting INTO the locked winter peak — high skill).
- Start Jan–Mar → **0.17–0.19** (forecasting ACROSS spring — skill COLLAPSES).
The spring barrier: forecasts crossing boreal spring lose skill because the system RESETS there. Fastest
month-to-month motion is April (|Δ| 0.265) = the pump charging the new cycle.

## 3. This resolves the rung-coupling null
ENSO's octave rungs (8/16/32/64/128mo) don't lock to EACH OTHER (only 16–32 weakly) because they're all
**slaved to the external orbit clock** — no internal coupling needed; the annual cycle paces them, spring is the
reset valve. The earlier hunt for an internal clock was looking in the wrong place; the clock is the planet's
orbit.

## Honest framing

## 4. Where the orbit-coupling lives: the EVENT, not the rung (phase tests, 14 Jun)
Tested how each rung couples to the 12-mo sun clock.
- **Energy modulation:** only the fast **8-mo** rung is significantly seasonally paced (mod 4.8%, peaks Jan, p=0.02);
  interannual rungs 16–128mo show ~0% energy-seasonal-modulation (the 12-mo clock sits next to the 8-mo band).
- **Phase-of-peak (event timing):** the **RAW** NINO3.4 events are STRONGLY winter-locked — warm peaks cluster in
  **Dec (R=0.32, p=6e-5)**, cold troughs **Dec (R=0.36, p=1e-5)**. But **no single rung carries it**: 8/16mo
  uniform; 32mo marginal (R=0.23, p=0.057, prefers Nov); 64/128mo lean Nov but weak. All interannual rungs lean
  winter directionally, none individually significant.

**Resolution:** the orbit clock grips ENSO at the **EVENT level** — the coherent broadband warm/cold event peaks in
Dec (p~1e-5) — NOT at any single octave rung. The clock organises the rungs' ALIGNMENT into a winter-peaking event;
the per-rung decomposition spreads the locking out. So the sun clock acts on the whole oscillation's phase, the
coherent event, which the octave/per-rung view cannot localise. (Framework lesson: clock-coupling can live in the
combination, not the components — cf. "1+1=3", the relation/event is the carrier.)

## Whole-arc summary
1. ENSO octave rungs do NOT couple/phase-lock to each other (only 16–32 weakly; no 64–128 orbit; no φ-phase).
2. They are all paced by the EXTERNAL orbit clock: amplitude 1.70× winter/spring; spring = reset/pump (persistence
   0.78→0.18 across spring).
3. The clock grips at the EVENT level (Dec peak, p~1e-5), not per-rung. The clock is external (the planet's orbit),
   and it times the coherent event, not the bands.

## 5. Does the wet recoil DAMPEN the oncoming El Nino? NULL (recoil vs recharge, 14 Jun)
Dylan's hypothesis: the pre-El-Nino wet (departing La Nina recoil) is a reverse-dampening that reduces the
oncoming El Nino's "super effects." Test: 36 El Nino peaks (ONI>0.5), prior La Nina depth in [-24,-4]mo.
- **corr(prior recoil depth, El Nino peak) = +0.004** — FLAT. El Ninos after deep recoil peak +1.37 vs shallow
  +1.30 (no diff); after real La Nina +1.32 vs +1.39 (no diff). **Neither damping nor recharge in amplitude.**
- Weak: rebound RATE vs peak corr +0.24 (faster snap-back slightly bigger = mild recharge-flavour, OPPOSITE of
  damping, weak).
- **The El Nino's magnitude is DECOUPLED from the prior recoil depth.** The "recoil spring" (ledger) is a
  WITHIN-event restoring force (bounds/turns one oscillation, h=12 amplitude fix) — it does NOT create an
  event-to-event amplitude link. The current wet is not measurably dampening the coming El Nino's size.
- Limit: tested SST recoil (NINO3.4); the recharge pathway runs through subsurface heat content (WWV), not
  available here — so this rules out SST-recoil->amplitude, not WWV-recharge in general. Real ENSO (June 2026):
  El Nino underway, NINO3.4 +0.92C (wk 14 Jun), forecast moderate-strong.
