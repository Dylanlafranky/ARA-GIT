Universal Cascade — Residual Diagnostic

After two failed mechanic hypotheses (snapback, quarter-flip), measure the error directly instead of guessing what's missing. The shape of the residual will tell us what kind of mechanic the cascade actually needs.

Forecast horizon:

1. Actual vs Prediction (the amplitude story)

If the cascade is squashing amplitude, the blue prediction will look like a quieter version of the black actual — same swings, smaller range.

2. Residual by actual quartile

If the cascade is amplitude-squashing, low-actual quartile will show negative mean residual (cascade predicts too high) and high-actual quartile will show positive mean residual (cascade predicts too low). Symmetric bias = mean-regression.

3. FFT of residual — where does the error live?

Vertical lines mark the framework's φ-rungs (48mo dom_P × φ^k). If error sits ON the rungs, the cascade is under-using those rungs. If error sits BETWEEN, we're missing a non-φ frequency.

4. Residual autocorrelation

Random noise = corr drops to 0 immediately. Slow-decay = residual has memory and is NOT noise. Negative dip at lag ~24mo = residual itself oscillates like ENSO (same structure left over).

5. Residual by wall distance

0 = at center of historical range, 1 = at the wall. If errors are worst at walls, mechanic should target walls. If worst at center, mechanic should target transitions.
