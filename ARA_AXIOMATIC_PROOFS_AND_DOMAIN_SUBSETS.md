## 0. Reading rule and claim classes

ARA stands for **Accumulation–Release Asymmetry**. It proposes that a coupled
identity can be represented as a wave or sphere and minimally measured by one
oriented diameter running from `0` to `2`. The diameter is a relational cut of
the whole object, not the whole object itself.

This document uses five claim classes:

- **Definition** fixes the framework's vocabulary or a mathematical object.
- **Exact consequence** follows from the stated definitions.
- **Typed crosswalk** rewrites an established domain relation in ARA
  coordinates; it does not independently prove universal ARA.
- **Conditional hypothesis** follows only if an additional ARA modelling claim
  is true.
- **Empirical result** reports what a declared test supported, rejected or left
  unresolved.

The core mathematical claim is modest:

> ARA supplies a consistent family of reversible relational coordinates for
> two-pole identities when the orientation, scale, boundary, activity and
> retained relations are declared.

The larger claim—that all of nature is generated by this fractal wave/sphere
geometry—is empirical and remains open.
