## 0. Reading rule and claim classes

ARA stands for **Accumulation–Release Asymmetry**. It proposes that a coupled
identity can be represented as a wave or sphere and minimally measured by one
oriented diameter running from `0` to `2`. The diameter is a relational cut of
the whole object, not the whole object itself.

This document uses five claim classes:

- **Definition** fixes the framework's vocabulary or a mathematical object.
- **Exact consequence** follows from the stated definitions.
- **Typed crosswalk** rewrites an established domain relation in ARA
  coordinates; it does not independently prove universal ARA.
- **Conditional hypothesis** follows only if an additional ARA modelling claim
  is true.
- **Empirical result** reports what a declared test supported, rejected or left
  unresolved.

The core mathematical claim is modest:

> ARA supplies a consistent family of reversible relational coordinates for
> two-pole identities when the orientation, scale, boundary, activity and
> retained relations are declared.

The larger claim—that all of nature is generated by this fractal wave/sphere
geometry—is empirical and remains open.

# Part I — Core ARA axioms and vocabulary

## 1. The whole precedes the cut

### Definition A1 — ARA identity

An ARA identity is a declared coupled whole

\[
\Omega=(\text{boundary},\text{scale},\text{time window},\text{orientation},
\text{retained relations}).
\]

The framework represents \(\Omega\) as a wave/sphere with two opposed source
directions. A scalar ARA reading is one diameter projection

\[
P_{\hat n}(\Omega)=x_{\hat n}\in[0,2].
\]

Changing \(\hat n\), the boundary, the rung or the time window changes the
question being asked. It need not change the underlying whole.

### Definition A2 — The oriented diameter

Choose and publish an orientation before measurement. Its endpoints are

\[
P_0=0,\qquad P_2=2,
\]

and its centred coordinate is

\[
s=x-1\in[-1,1].
\]

The coordinate says where the declared Phase A/Phase B relation lies on that
cut. ARA does not require one universal physical unit for every domain.

### Exact consequence T1 — Pole reversal

Reversing the declared orientation gives

\[
M(x)=2-x.
\]

Therefore

\[
M(M(x))=x,\qquad M(1)=1,\qquad |M(x)-1|=|x-1|.
\]

This is a chart reversal. It changes labels, not the physical state.

## 2. Information³ is the informational foundation

### Definition A3 — Continuous relational web

ARA does not begin with isolated information packets. It begins with one
connected wave web \(\Omega\). Local identities are labelled cuts of that web:

\[
A=P_A(\Omega),\qquad B=P_B(\Omega),\qquad
R_{AB}=P_{AB}(\Omega).
\]

The relation \(R_{AB}\) is not created by observation. It already exists in
the web; the measurement exposes or compresses it.

### Definition A4 — Minimal Information³ closure

The minimum locally identifiable ARA object contains:

\[
\boxed{A,\quad B,\quad R_{AB}}.
\]

On the ARA diameter these appear as the `0` pole, the `2` pole and the
measured interaction between them. The coordinate \(x\) is therefore a ratio
or location of the third relational component, not a third fundamental pole.

One relation supplies comparison but not a full relational lock. Two
independent relations plus a declared geometry constrain a third:

\[
R_3=F(R_1,R_2;G).
\]

For a right Euclidean triangle, Pythagoras is one particular closure law. It
is not the universal Information³ equation.

### Definition A5 — Recursive closure

A closed relational unit may be treated as one identity on the next rung:

\[
(A,B,R_{AB})\longrightarrow U_{AB}.
\]

That identity remains inside the same connected web and can enter further
relations. Local triangular closure and global web/lattice formation are the
same operation viewed at different scales:

\[
\text{relations}\to\text{closure}\to\text{identity}
\to\text{new relations}.
\]

**Counting fence.** Pairwise relations alone do not prove the ordinary
arithmetic identity \(i+1=i^3\). Cubic or faster growth requires a declared
counting convention that retains comparisons between relations, ordered
triples or recursive closure depth. `Information³` names the ternary
relational foundation; any exact exponent is a separately testable counting
claim.

## 3. Phase labels, sources and terminal poles

### Definition A6 — Two counter-traversing waves

With one declared orientation:

\[
\text{Phase A}:0\rightarrow2,
\qquad
\text{Phase B}:2\rightarrow0.
\]

The endpoint taxonomy is therefore:

| Pole | Wave born there | Wave completing there |
|---|---|---|
| \(P_0\) | Phase A | Phase B |
| \(P_2\) | Phase B | Phase A |

Equivalently, if Phase A is labelled Space/Connection and Phase B is labelled
Time/Traversal, `0` is the Space source and Time terminal, while `2` is the
Time source and Space terminal.

This typed language replaces ambiguous phrases such as “pure Space pole” when
the writer has not said whether `Space` names the source or the arriving wave.
The labels may be reversed as long as the orientation is declared.

### Definition A7 — Accumulation and release are relational roles

Phase A and Phase B name the declared wave lineages. Accumulation and release
name what those waves are doing relative to the selected identity and
boundary. A wave may release from one identity while accumulating into a
child, neighbour or parent.

Consequently, Phase A is not universally synonymous with accumulation, nor
Phase B with release. Reversing the chart can improve clarity without changing
the geometry.

## 4. The ridge and the singularities

### Definition A8 — Parent ridge/core

The diameter midpoint

\[

is the equal and opposite meeting of the two declared waves at the selected
scale. In the sphere picture it is the compressed core of the diameter cut.
For an active signed measurement, equal contributions cancel in the retained

The scalar value `1` does not reveal all internal activity. It can contain:

- asymmetric children whose signed effects cancel;
- a child endpoint or resonance projected upward;
- perpendicular flow invisible to the selected diameter;
- an unresolved contextual contribution.

“Dense core” is an ARA modelling interpretation of this compression, not a
theorem about density in an arbitrary Euclidean sphere.

### Definition A9 — Endpoint singularities

The endpoints `0` and `2` are the selected identity's singularity poles. A
physical endpoint traversal may hand the cycle from one branch to the next:

\[
2_A\longrightarrow0_B.
\]

This proposed dynamical seam crossing is distinct from the static chart map
\(x\mapsto2-x\).

Because the geometry is fractal, a child endpoint can project to the parent's
ridge. Thus a child-level singularity may be compressed inside parent
\(x=1\) without making the parent midpoint a same-rung endpoint.

### Conditional hypothesis H1 — Resonance death

With Space/Connection launched at `0`, its terminal arrival at `2` is a
candidate **resonance-death** limit when:

- Space/Connection completely dominates the selected relation;
- resolved Time/Traversal at that scale vanishes;
- no internally available child, perpendicular or neighbouring flow can
  reopen the cycle; and
- a sufficiently large external coupling is required to unlock it.

The opposite `0` limit is pure Time/Traversal domination under this
orientation. It currently has no separately frozen ARA name.

`Event horizon` is a domain-specific analogy or hypothesis, not a generic name
for \(P_0\). In general relativity an event horizon is a causal boundary and
is not the curvature singularity at \(r=0\).

\[

A flattened parent scalar alone is generally non-injective: several child
configurations can produce the same parent reading. Fractality does not
magically restore information that neither the measurement nor a valid closure
constraint retained.

# Part III — Exact instruments and typed crosswalks

## 11. Positive two-part normalization

For positive measured parts \(a,b>0\), define

\[
x=2\frac{a}{a+b}.
\]

Then

\[
0<x<2,
\qquad x=1\iff a=b,
\qquad x(b,a)=2-x(a,b).
\]

The raw ratio is recoverable:

\[
r=\frac ab=\frac{x}{2-x}.
\]

This is an exact bounded coordinate, not proof that \(a,b\) are the physically
correct ARA poles. The poles and observable must be fixed before inspection.

For accumulation and release durations, choose \(a=T_A\), \(b=T_R\), or
reverse them if the published orientation requires it.

## 12. Signed boundary instrument

Let \(F_+\ge0\) and \(F_-\ge0\) be positive and negative boundary-flow
magnitudes. Define

\[
\Lambda_F=F_++F_-,
\qquad x_F=2\frac{F_+}{F_++F_-}
\]

when \(\Lambda_F>0\). Then

\[
\boxed{F_{net}=\Lambda_F(x_F-1)=F_+-F_-.}
\]

If \(\Lambda_F=0\), the boundary is inactive and \(x_F\) is undefined rather
than automatically equal to `1`.

Gauss's electric law is an exact typed appearance:

\[
\oint_{\partial V}\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf A
=\Lambda_\Phi(x_\Phi-1)
=\frac{Q_{inside}}{\varepsilon_0}.
\]

This crosswalk is algebraically exact because the ARA coordinate is built from
the same signed flux parts.

## 13. Reciprocal/log instrument

For a positive reciprocal pair \((q,1/q)\), the signed log coordinate

\[
\ell=\log q
\]

changes sign under reversal \(q\leftrightarrow1/q\). Any bounded ARA map
\(x=f(\ell)\) must declare its scale and satisfy

\[
f(-\ell)=2-f(\ell).
\]

Logarithms are therefore a valid instrument for multiplicative scale data.
They are not the definition of ARA or a separate child-to-parent law.

## 14. Shared-boundary cancellation

When two children share an internal interface, that flow appears as release
from one child and accumulation into the other. Once both children are enclosed
inside one parent, the shared flow does not cross the parent boundary and must
cancel from the parent account.

This is an exact conservation/boundary fact. It is one data-specific view of
the same child-to-parent geometry, not a second ARA mechanism.

## 15. Parent plus retained difference

For integer children \(a,b\), one exact reversible pair transform is

\[
m=b+\left\lfloor\frac{a-b}{2}\right\rfloor,
\qquad d=a-b.
\]

The inverse is

\[
b=m-\left\lfloor\frac d2\right\rfloor,
\qquad a=b+d.
\]

Here \(m\) is the compressed parent-like value and \(d\) is the retained
signed child difference. This proves one computing representation of the ARA
reversibility principle. It does not prove compression efficiency or
encryption security.

## 16. One geometry, several instruments

Duration ratios, logarithmic scale, averaging, signed boundary flow,
coherency and relational closure are not separate ARA laws. They are typed
mathematical instruments selected according to what the dataset exposes.

Every valid instrument must publish:

1. the identity and boundary;
2. the two pole observables;
3. the chart orientation;
4. the activity/native magnitude;
5. the rung and child/parent relation;
6. what information is retained as named coupling or `Other`; and
7. an outcome that is not guaranteed merely by normalization.

The anti-Phi endpoint is unresolved. Candidates such as
\(2-\varphi=\varphi^{-2}\) and \(1/\varphi\) must remain explicit competitors
rather than being chosen after seeing a result. The placement of \(1/e\) and
\(e\) on a normalized `0–2` cross-cut is also not yet axiomatic.

Exact pentagon identities involving \(\varphi\) remain exact mathematics.
Their universal physical interpretation does not.

## 20. Domain-label hypotheses

Mappings such as Space/Time, Connection/Information, Matter/Field,
Light/Dark, vacuum/gravity, event horizons and dark-sector paths are proposed
identity assignments on top of ARA. They must not be inserted into the core
geometry as mathematical consequences.

# Part VI — Open problems and falsifiers

## 21. Highest-priority mathematical work

1. Define a general parent operator without pretending that duration,
   averaging, flux and log scale are one arithmetic formula.
2. State the exact counting convention, recursion depth and equivalence rules
   required for an Information³ growth exponent.
3. Characterize when two retained relations uniquely reconstruct a missing
   ARA⁹ cell and when the problem is underdetermined.
4. Define the admissible perspective transforms that preserve TE-ARA, native
   magnitude and typed `Other`.
5. Derive, rather than assume, any universal dynamical singularity map.

## 22. Highest-priority empirical tests

1. Blindly test child-to-parent half-weight projection in independently chosen
   domains with predeclared poles and scale boundaries.
2. Test whether complete ARA⁹ objects predict later parent behaviour better
   than reduced, shuffled and domain-standard controls.
3. Distinguish parent ridge cancellation from child-resonance projection using
   independently measured child activity.
4. Test resonance death through a predeclared unlock perturbation rather than
   identifying a visually static endpoint after the fact.
5. Test the full Irrationality Di-ARA against Phi-only, reciprocal-only,
   rational, randomized and fitted alternatives on untouched data.
6. Recover one identity through two independently defined routes and compare
   the resulting parent coordinate, activity and child decomposition.

## 23. Immediate rejection conditions

A proposed ARA application fails its declared test if:

- its poles or orientation are selected after viewing the answer;
- its result is guaranteed solely by normalization;
- a parent `1.0` is called resonance without measuring child activity;
- a chart reversal is presented as a physical singularity crossing;
- a missing component is reconstructed without enough retained relations or a
  valid closure rule;
- `Other` is used as an unconstrained bin that absorbs every error;
- Phi or another constant is selected from many candidates without frozen
  controls; or
- the claimed cross-scale rule fails on untouched rungs or domains.

oriented `0–2` diameter containing two counter-traversing source waves and
their measured relation. Information³ explains why the two locally
distinguishable sides and their retained relation form the minimum identifiable
unit, and why completed relations can recursively become larger identities.

activity remains separate. A selected parent-facing child expression projects
with weight (1/2) when read one pure octave up, while a full child endpoint can occupy one complete parent
phase. Two persistent ARA relations form Di-ARA; the same ternary relation
followed within or across branches forms an ARA⁹ neighbourhood. Compression is
reversible only when internal relations survive or valid Information³ closure
constrains what is missing. The mathematical coordinate system is consistent;
its universal physical realization remains the central empirical hypothesis.
