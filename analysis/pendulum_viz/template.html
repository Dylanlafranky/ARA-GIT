Re-run audit — what held, what moved

After the ARA-framework audit, scripts were re-run on real data. Six of seven reproduced their original numbers; one (leadership) exposed two artifacts under a stricter turn-detector.

Element 1 — each arm's own geometry

Phase portraits (angle vs angular velocity, colour = time) and spectra. All three share one dominant period (1.333 s); the rung ladder is in amplitude, not frequency.
Amplitude ratios A2/A1, A3/A2, A3/A1 reported raw. No directional handover is defined here, so φ is not invoked — these are not a φ claim.

Element 3 — relational ARA between arms (the bends)

Each joint read as a relation: bend = 1 + wrap(θ_lower − θ_upper)/π on rest-relative angles, so inline = the 1.0 ridge. Upper joint hugs the ridge; the lower joint swings far wider.

Leadership & rung dominance claim held 3/3 upper-two structure demoted

Who turns first on each big swing (prominence-filtered turns). The bottom rung (arm-3) leads most often and holds the longest single block in every run. The earlier "upper two split evenly" did not survive — arm-2/arm-1 are erratic.

Coupling — arm-2 is the shared carrier (the clock is the common mode)

All pairs are phase-locked (PLV). Raw corr(arm1,arm3) is positive, but partial correlation holding arm-2 fixed flips negative in 2/3 runs: arm-2 carries the shared common mode the ends both ride — it is the carrier, not a proven causal mediator.
A partial-corr sign-flip identifies a common carrier, not mediation. The clock is the shared ~1.333 s common mode; no single arm is the timekeeper (arm-2 isn't even reliably the steadiest).

Element 4 — reconstruction (SVD / data modes) ARA-inspired reading of a standard POD

The 3-arm motion compresses to two modes (≈99.4% of variance): a common mode (all same sign, weights = the amplitude ladder) and a 1-vs-3 anti-phase mode.

Element 5a — strictly-causal forecast (and the honest baseline)

Train half fits modes + weights; test half held out. Skill looks high, but the one-period-ago baseline is flat ~0.98 and ties/beats the model — this is a quasi-periodic clock, not forecasting chaos. Persistence (last value) is useless, confirming period-ago is the right yardstick.

Element 5b — predict the deepest arm from the calm ones

Hold arm-3 out entirely and predict it, strictly causally, from only arms 1–2's past. It reaches corr 0.99 and beats arm-3's own self-AR at 2 s — the deepest arm is slaved to the shallower structure ("look up, not down").

The two waves — fast carrier & slow decay envelope added — your catch

The 1.333 s swing (carrier) rides inside a slowly shrinking amplitude envelope. The whole 60 s run is one accumulation→release arc settling back toward the 1.0 rest ridge — the slow wave is monotonic decay (friction), not a second oscillation. Pick an arm; arm-3 (bottom) sheds amplitude fastest.

Provenance: every number recomputed from the real triple-pendulum runs (3 free-swing recordings, decimated for display).
