# The rung staircase — two fully-worked jumps (1 Jul 2026)

**Scripts:** `scurve_rungjump_test.py` (jump 1), `jump2_rotation_test.py` (jump 2), `rung_staircase_viz.py`.
**Figures:** `rungjump_anatomy.png`, `jump2_rotation.png`, `rung_staircase.png`. **Replicable/deterministic**, all values sourced.

Dylan's claim: the S-curve is one rung jump — base units stall at a pole; an emergent HIGHER component (built from the assembled lower units) crosses the octave; it maxes out; **rinse and repeat** with a new higher component.

## Jump 1 — strength → gravity  (shape rung)
- Base units = grain strength: **stuck**. Below the transition, size↔roundness ρ=−0.03 (p=0.91) — growing bigger does nothing; scattered by material (rubble vs monolith).
- Higher component = **self-gravity** (emergent from assembled mass). It overtakes material strength (~10 MPa) at the observed roundness transition (166 km; crossover band 42–338 km for 1–100 MPa).
- Above: size drives roundness ρ=+0.78 (p<0.001), tapering to the max = a sphere.

## Jump 2 — gravity → spin  (rotation rung)  [the "rinse and repeat"]
Higher-component ratio m = ω²a³/GM = centrifugal ÷ gravity (exact analog of gravity ÷ strength). 12 gravity-rounded bodies, NASA Planetary Fact Sheet + Haumea occultation (a=1161, c=513 km, P=3.92 h).
- Base state = hydrostatic **sphere**: **stuck**. Slow rotators (m<0.01, n=6) all f≈0.002 — spheres regardless of size/mass.
- Higher component = **spin**. Flattening tracks spin: **ρ(f,m)=+0.96**, but ρ(f,size)=+0.15 and ρ(f,mass)=+0.14 — the spin does the crossing, not the base gravity/size. Bodies follow the Maclaurin f≈1.25m line.
- Taper/max = rotational breakup at **m~1** (centrifugal=gravity at the equator). **Haumea sits at m=1.16** — a Jacobi (triaxial) ellipsoid that has shed a ring and two moons. The literal max.

## Verdict
Both jumps show the identical structure — stuck-at-a-pole → an emergent higher component (assembled from the lower units) overtakes the incumbent force at a crossover → taper to a max → the next rung takes over with a NEW higher component. Strength→gravity→spin is a two-step staircase, each step the same shape. Dylan's "rinse and repeat" is confirmed on real, sourced data across two independent physical rungs.

## Fences
Jump-1 shoulder *asymmetry* was not identifiable (n=29; the stuck-plateau-then-sharp-jump structure is the robust content). Jump-2 planets sit below the uniform-fluid Maclaurin line due to central mass condensation (known). Order-of-magnitude strengths; canonical constants. n small — suggestive, but the correlations and crossovers are clean.
