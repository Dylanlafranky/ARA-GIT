# PN35 post-hoc Phi-to-pentagon conversion note

**Date:** 22 July 2026  
**Status:** post-hoc hypothesis; not part of PN35's frozen success rule

## Dylan's observation

After seeing the frozen-model comparison, Dylan identified a different reading from the registered direct-crossing
claim: Phi may be the moving/time handover wave, while pentagonal geometry is the structured appearance produced by
its conversion. On this reading, testing whether primes sit directly on the raw Phi carrier asks for the wrong
endpoint.

## What the PN35 result visibly contains

| Frozen rule | Rotation | Lane-stratified AUC |
|---|---:|---:|
| 36-degree shear | `1/10` turn | `0.503130` |
| Pentagon | `1/5` turn | `0.501258` |
| Golden/Phi carrier | `1/phi^2` turn | `0.497180` |

The top two positive point estimates are therefore the half-pentagon and pentagon rotations, while the raw golden
carrier is below chance. That pattern is **descriptively compatible** with a carrier-to-structured-image distinction.

It is not yet evidence for conversion. PN35 compared three independent constant rotations; it did not define or
measure an operator that takes the state of a Phi carrier as input and emits a pentagonal state as output. In
addition, all effects are small: the 256 circular-shift AUC range was `[0.493504, 0.504219]`, which contains both
pentagonal point estimates.

## The new testable object

The next object, if pursued, is not:

> primes are closest to a constant Phi crossing.

It is:

> a predeclared Phi carrier is converted or quantised into a five-sector structural state, and prime incidence is
> associated with a specific part of that conversion—such as sector entry, sector handover or closure—more strongly
> than with the raw carrier, a direct pentagon rotation and matched non-fivefold quantisations.

A clean future test would need, before new prime labels are opened:

1. one explicit map `C_(Phi->5)` from continuous Phi phase to fivefold structural state;
2. one declared event within that map (entry, handover, centre or closure);
3. fresh untouched octave rungs;
4. raw-Phi, direct-pentagon, 36-degree, other polygon and circular-shift controls; and
5. a requirement that the converted model beat both of its components, not merely chance.
