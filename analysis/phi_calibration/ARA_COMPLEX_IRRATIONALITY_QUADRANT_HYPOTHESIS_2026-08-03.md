## Original physical intuition retained

The motivating ARA intuition is that nature may preserve identities through
an efficient tension between two useful but dangerous extremes:

- connection-heavy, rational recurrence helps a structure reinforce and
  survive, but exact closure repeated across all relevant scales can remove
  the asymmetry needed for continued traversal; the proposed terminal limit
  is called **resonance death** in ARA;
- information-heavy non-repetition avoids that lock, but unstructured
  randomness need not preserve lineage or make earlier information
  recoverable.

The proposed useful middle is **structured non-repetition**: sufficiently
non-closing to maintain movement, yet sufficiently rule-governed to retain
information pathways. Phyllotaxis is one motivating example of this kind of
efficient sharing, but its occurrence does not prove that the same numerical
constant governs every scale or domain.

The cross-scale extension is also a hypothesis: a controlled mismatch at one
rung may prevent exact resonance from propagating into a larger cascade of
closed rungs. This possible protection against cross-scale resonance collapse
requires direct tests; it is not assumed as fact.

## Exact mathematical scaffold

Let one two-cut relation be represented as a complex state

\[
z(t)=r_0 e^{(\sigma+i\omega)t}
=r_0e^{\sigma t}e^{i\omega t}.
\]

Then

\[
|z(t)|=r_0e^{\sigma t},
\qquad
\arg z(t)=\omega t.
\]

The signs of the two independent rates give four modes:

| radial sign | phase sign | canonical form | local reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| \(\sigma<0\) | \(\omega>0\) | \(e^{(-|\sigma|+i|\omega|)t}\) | contracting, forward |
| \(\sigma<0\) | \(\omega<0\) | \(e^{(-|\sigma|-i|\omega|)t}\) | contracting, reverse |
| \(\sigma>0\) | \(\omega>0\) | \(e^{(|\sigma|+i|\omega|)t}\) | expanding, forward |
| \(\sigma>0\) | \(\omega<0\) | \(e^{(|\sigma|-i|\omega|)t}\) | expanding, reverse |

Writing `1` in place of `|sigma|` is only a normalized sign example. It does
not assert that every observed system has unit growth or decay rate.

For sampled data, the exact local form is

\[
q_n=\frac{z_{n+1}}{z_n}=s_ne^{i\delta_n},
\]

\[
\sigma_n=\frac{\log s_n}{\Delta t},
\qquad
\omega_n=\frac{\delta_n}{\Delta t}.
\]

This is the operational object to measure. The four quadrants are generated
by `sign(log s_n)` and `sign(delta_n)`.

## Where rationality and irrationality enter

For a fixed sampled phase step

\[
\rho=\frac{\delta}{2\pi},
\]

a rational `rho` eventually returns to exactly the same finite set of phase
positions. An irrational `rho` never closes exactly. Random motion is a third
case: it may also fail to close, but without one stable rotation rule and its
recoverable near-return structure.

Therefore the present hierarchy is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{radial contraction/expansion}
\times
\text{forward/reverse phase}
}
\]

followed by

\[
\boxed{
\text{rational closure}
\;\text{vs}\;
\text{structured irrational progression}
\;\text{vs}\;
\text{random progression}
}
\]

inside the phase coordinate.

This corrects the earlier attempt to make “rationality” and “randomness” the
missing geometric opposites of Phi. They are closure classes, not the two
orientation signs of the primary quadrant.
