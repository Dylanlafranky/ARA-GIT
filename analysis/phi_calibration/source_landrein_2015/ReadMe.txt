Measurements of Phyllotaxis from Landrein et al, 2015 J.Exp.Bot

For each genotype, there are two files, one with the calculated angles of divergence between siliques and one with the angular position (raw data, "Angles-Bruts" in French) measured on the apparatus and from which the angles of divergence were calculated.

There are four 4 genotypes: Col0 and WS-4 (two different Arabidopsis ecotypes), and clasp1 (mutant for CLASP protein in Col-0 background) and bot1-7 (mutant for katanin in a WS-4 background), analyzed in two different growth conditions: Long day directly (JL: "jours longs" in French) and Short day then Long day (JC-JL: "jours courts, jours longs).
