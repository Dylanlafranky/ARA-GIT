# T303 — Phi / three-eighths state-duality audit

**Registered:** 30 July 2026, before searching the numerical ARA⁹ result
artifacts for `3/8`, `0.375`, or a Phi-versus-three-eighths comparison.  
**Status:** retrospective repository audit plus candidate-test design  
**Originating interpretation:** Dylan La Franchi

## 1. Proposed rule

The post-T302 interpretation is:

\[
\underbrace{\frac38}_{\substack{\text{connection-heavy}\\
\text{local closing child}}}
\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
\underbrace{\phi^{-2}}_{\substack{\text{motion-heavy}\\
\text{non-closing parent carrier}}}.
\]

T302 supplied the motivating split: `3/8` won its frozen local-step endpoint,
while exact Phi won its frozen cumulative-carrier endpoint. This audit asks
whether earlier “Phi hunting” results become more coherent when those two
states are distinguished.

## 2. Classification locked before result search

Classification is determined from the artifact's already-written question,
coordinate and title, not from whichever constant fits afterward.

### Connection / closure

An artifact belongs here only when its pre-existing primary object is a
static or locally recurring:

- lattice;
- connected tensor or relation matrix;
- locked/closed child;
- retained structural occupancy;
- local placement step.

### Motion / carrier

An artifact belongs here only when its pre-existing primary object is:

- an ordered trajectory;
- cumulative position;
- transport or rotation;
- a handover time/path;
- a moving larger-wave envelope.

### Hybrid or ineligible

An artifact is not used for a directional pass when:

- its primary object deliberately mixes connection and motion;
- the relevant label was assigned after seeing the fitted value;
- Phi or `3/8` was built into preprocessing, simulation, normalization,
  candidate selection or data generation;
- no common dimensionless coordinate exists;
- the result reports only a picture without an auditable numerical object.

Hybrid and ineligible artifacts remain in the inventory; they are not silently
discarded.

## 3. Evidence levels

Every apparent match must be labelled:

1. **Direct measured comparison:** the same independently measured coordinate
   was scored against both `3/8` and \(\phi^{-2}\), with fixed rivals.
2. **Recomputable comparison:** an existing raw coordinate permits the two
   distances to be calculated without introducing a fitted transform.
3. **Structural analogy:** the result has a rational lattice/closure or an
   irrational carrier shape but no common scalar comparison.
4. **Forced:** the value follows from construction, normalization, an assigned
   mirror, a selected candidate, or a supplied generator.

Only levels 1–2 can support the numerical state-duality rule.

## 5. ARA⁹ lattice-count fence

The canonical ARA⁹ relation object contains nine cells. An ideal Bell
connected tensor has three nonzero principal directions. Therefore:

\[
\frac{3}{9}=\frac13,
\]

not `3/8`.

No cell may be removed merely to manufacture an eight-cell denominator.
If a distinct, pre-existing eight-neighbour lattice boundary exists, it must
be named independently and the treatment of its central cell fixed before
scoring. A general “three visible features out of eight” resemblance is not
numerical evidence.

### Post-audit target clarification from Dylan

The intended ARA⁹ Phi/`3/8` object is **not a count inside the static
nine-cell lattice**. The complete connected lattice is one identity at a
time slice. The target is its **meta movement across successive time
slices**:

\[
C(t_0)\longrightarrow C(t_1)\longrightarrow C(t_2)\longrightarrow\cdots .
\]

The `3/9 = 1/3` fence remains valid against an accidental occupancy claim,
but it does not test or reject this meta-trajectory hypothesis. A valid test
must follow the whole lattice's scale-free orientation/path and preserve its
local closure as a separate coordinate.

## 6. Primary audit questions

1. Does an independently measured ARA⁹ connection coordinate lie closer to
   `3/8` than to Phi?
2. Does an independently measured ARA⁹ trajectory/carrier coordinate lie
   closer to Phi than to `3/8`?
3. Are the two comparisons made in commensurate units?
4. Does the proposed distinction resolve an old Phi failure without changing
   that test's frozen verdict?
5. Is any apparent ARA⁹ `3/8` simply the result of three axes, eight
   neighbours, a normalized determinant, or another construction?

## 7. Broader Phi-result audit

After the ARA⁹ audit, inventory only the strongest earlier results that have:

- a saved protocol or report;
- recoverable data or numerical output;
- an explicit Phi claim;
- a fixed control or rival;
- a pre-existing connection/motion classification.

The first candidates are T299–T302, PN35–PN36, Golden Stars, and the
strict-causal Phi forecast ablations. Exploratory archived scripts may inform
provenance but cannot decide the result.

## 8. Verdict

This first audit is not allowed to claim universal support from a tally of
heterogeneous files.

- **Supported for ARA⁹:** at least one level-1/2 connection comparison favours
  `3/8` and at least one level-1/2 motion comparison favours Phi, each with
  fixed rivals and no forced transform.
- **Suggestive:** the directions agree but one side is structural-only,
  post hoc, or lacks an independent rival comparison.
- **Not supported:** the directions reverse, disappear under controls, or are
  construction artifacts.
- **Not testable from existing records:** no commensurate independent
  coordinate exists.

All earlier frozen verdicts remain unchanged.
