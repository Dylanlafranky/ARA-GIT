## Intended reading

For a trajectory \(H(t)\), the amplitude coordinate says how full the unresolved identity is. The rate coordinate
says whether it is opening or closing. Their two-dimensional path distinguishes states that have the same
amplitude but opposite direction.

The resulting quadrants are:

- low amplitude / opening;
- high amplitude / opening;
- high amplitude / closing;
- low amplitude / closing.

Loop closure, spiralling, multiple lobes and centre drift are reported as geometry. They are not forced into the
data.
