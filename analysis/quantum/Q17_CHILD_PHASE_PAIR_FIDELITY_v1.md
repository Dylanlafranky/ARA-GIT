\(r_i^S=\mu_i^S-M^S\):

\[
\text{opposition}_{ij}^S
=
-\cos(r_i^S,r_j^S),
\]

\[
\text{balance}_{ij}^S
=
\frac{2\min(\lVert r_i^S\rVert,\lVert r_j^S\rVert)}
{\lVert r_i^S\rVert+\lVert r_j^S\rVert},

\[
\text{closure error}_{ij}^S
=
\frac{\left\lVert(\mu_i^S+\mu_j^S)/2-M^S\right\rVert}

when the pair midpoint returns to the parent ridge.
