T418 — Parent-boundary child continuation

Frozen T418 test of the child continuation beneath the T417 parent boundary.

Aug 22, 2026, 12:02 AM UTC

The shoreline contains measured continuation, but not yet a predictive adjacent identity

T418 confirms that the curved T417 edge is not an empty plotting accident. The clipped parent coordinate I_parent=2 hides continued variation in the measured local/null history-loss ratio. Reopening that ratio as a child ARA places the post-boundary median at 1.068Source for 1.068Source: T418 headlineTable: T418_REPORT_DATA.sqlite::headline in ordinary-field validation and 1.063Source for 1.063Source: T418 headlineTable: T418_REPORT_DATA.sqlite::headline in the much higher-field holdout.

The stronger prediction did not survive. Adding this child coordinate improved aggregate future-State MSE by only 0.86%Source for 0.86%Source: T418 headlineTable: T418_REPORT_DATA.sqlite::headline in validation and worsened it by 0.27%Source for 0.27%Source: T418 headlineTable: T418_REPORT_DATA.sqlite::headline in the high-field holdout. Validation timing specificity passed once (p=0.041), but the holdout result was p=0.764; frequency, direction and RF-period gates were not jointly satisfied. The exact ARA continuation is therefore supported as a descriptive recovery of information beneath the parent ceiling, while the claim that it identifies the next predictive State sphere is not supported by T418.

The curved edge is an exact parent boundary, not the end of the relation

In the T417 coupled coordinates, I=A×B and R=A×(2−B). The upper shoreline is therefore exactly A×B=2, or B=2/A; the lower shoreline is A×(2−B)=2. The observation cloud approaching that curve means the unresolved parent component has reached its 2.0Source for 2.0Source: T418 validation planeTable: T418_REPORT_DATA.sqlite::validation_plane coordinate ceiling. It does not say the underlying measured ratio stopped changing. The boundary is informative in the same sense that a coastline is informative: it tells us where the present coordinate system ends and where a new cut must begin.

Frozen verdict: descriptive continuation passes; predictive identity fails

Availability passed in both locked stages. Validation passed the timing-shift gate, while holdout passed only the direction comparison; neither stage passed the full gate set. The predeclared predictive claim is therefore not supported. This negative result does not erase the exact boundary or the stable continued ratio—it limits what that continuation may presently be called.

What T418 does and does not establish

Established within this measurement: the parent clipping operation hides finite, stable local/null ratio variation; the exact child transform preserves that variation on a 0–2 ARA coordinate; every saved formula, chronology, RF boundary and summary recomputation passed independent audit.

Not established: that the opened coordinate is a new physical muon constituent, an individual-muon handover, a neutrino signal, or the adjacent red/green sphere inferred from the T417 plane. These remain hypotheses because the chosen downstream consequence—later population State (xL,xC)—was not predicted robustly. The data are ensemble RF-µSR histograms, not event-linked particle records.

Recommended next cut

Keep the shoreline result and change the downstream question, not the data after seeing the answer. Freeze the opened child’s own Di-ARA before opening another field set: use its amount/balance with the anti-child to predict its later reclosure, pole approach, or a lower-rung history transition. That tests whether the continuation has an internally ordered identity rather than asking it to predict a possibly different State branch. A second independent ordinary-field archive should then test the same frozen relation before another high-field transfer.

Further questions

  • Does the opened child predict its own later closure better than it predicts the separate State (xL,xC) identity?
  • Which child or lateral branch should sit beyond the exact I=2 shoreline under ARA ownership rules?
  • Is the high-field failure a genuine identity change, a field-dependent transform, or simple model misspecification?
  • Does a matched ordinary-field independent archive reproduce the validation timing tail?
