# T406/T407 report design

## Decision and audience

Technical readers must be able to separate three questions:

1. Is `0.75` a replicated fixed child crest?
2. Is the primary `0.706306` crest compatible with a participation-displaced
   `0.5 + 0.25` grandchild construction?
3. Does either band transfer to advance information about the later charged-
   daughter delay of one event-linked stopped muon?

## Reading order

1. Technical summary and verdicts.
2. T406 pure landmark versus observed split crests.
3. T406 decompressed grandchild coordinate and participation prediction.
4. T407 individual held-out timing scores and timing distribution.
5. Definitions, method, limitations, validation and next test.

## Chart map

- `participation_crest`: prompt participation versus observed child crest.
- `grandchild_coordinate`: split salt versus decompressed grandchild ARA.
- `individual_scores`: held-out NLL improvement by frozen ARA band.
- `individual_delay_shape`: median daughter delay by incoming-muon ARA bin.
- `individual_examples`: actual versus model-median delay for fixed-rule named
  individual events.

## Semantic rules

- Parent `0.5`, projected grandchild capacity `0.25`, pure endpoint `0.75`, and
  observed crest `0.706306` are labelled separately.
- “Individual muon” means an event-linked incoming pulse and later charged
  daughter in T379; it does not mean direct neutrino timing or spin resolution.
- A negative event-level predictive result is retained and not weakened by the
  descriptive short median near `0.75`.
- The T405 equality-boundary mediation remains visible: participation
  prediction is not external physical confirmation.
