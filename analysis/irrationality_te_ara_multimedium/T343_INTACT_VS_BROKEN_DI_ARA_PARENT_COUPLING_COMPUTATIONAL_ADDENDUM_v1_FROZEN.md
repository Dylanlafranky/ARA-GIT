## Why this correction was required

The implementation audit found a rung mismatch before scoring. In the T343
claim, the two children of the Irrationality Di-ARA are the two already-formed
ARA relations:

\[
a_t=\log s_t
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
b_t=\Delta\theta_t,
\]

not the domain-specific upstream measurements that happened to generate the
complex record `z_t`.

In framework language, `a` is the diameter/radial contraction–expansion wave
currently associated with the candidate `1/e ↔ e` relation, while `b` is the
perpendicular circumference/angular reverse–forward wave currently associated
with the open anti-Phi ↔ Phi hypothesis. The exact constants are not tested by
T343. Only their two-axis coupling is tested.

Breaking upstream sensor channels would move the experiment down another rung
and would not directly test whether these two declared ARA waves form a parent.

## Correct broken-pair construction

For each of the exactly `1,000` deterministic controls specified by the main
protocol:

1. keep every frozen domain, split, lineage block and sample count unchanged;
2. draw the frozen shift fraction and alternate the shifted axis as specified;
3. circularly shift `a` on odd controls and `b` on even controls, separately
   inside every block;
4. retain the other axis unchanged;
5. preserve each axis's values, native order up to cyclic origin, marginal
   distribution, cadence and block membership;
6. recompute only the broken four-region address from the shifted sign pair;
7. fit `P(Q_intact,t+1 | Q_broken,t)` on calibration and score the same intact
   holdout target.

The child-only predictors are correspondingly the radial sign
`R_t=1[a_t≥0]` and angular sign `C_t=1[b_t≥0]`. The intact parent remains their
four-state joint address.

No `z`, quotient or upstream source channel is re-estimated in a broken
control. Calibration origins and scales were already applied before `a` and
`b` entered the frozen T342/T343 block record.
