# T342 interpretive correction — originator clarification

**Date:** 5 August 2026  
**Applies to:** `T342-MULTIMEDIUM-IRRATIONALITY-TE-ARA-v1`  
**Status:** post-result scope correction; no frozen protocol, data, score or verdict was changed

## Why this correction exists

After viewing the T342 result geometrically, Dylan La Franchi identified that
the operationalisation had added a stronger rule than the ARA framework
intended. The frozen protocol asked whether every measured identity followed
one common neighbour-by-neighbour quadrant traversal grammar. That is not a
core Di-ARA claim.

The result of that registered auxiliary test remains unchanged:

- six domains were inferentially eligible;
- one domain passed both registered endpoints;
- the frozen auxiliary verdict remains **NOT SUPPORTED (`1/6`)**.

This correction changes the interpretation of that verdict, not the verdict.

## Originator's intended Di-ARA geometry

Di-ARA is two ARA relations coupled across perpendicular axes. Their joint
relation supplies four mixed regions:

\[
Ab,\qquad aB,\qquad bA,\qquad Ba.
\]

Capitalisation identifies the locally leading contribution; the letter order
preserves ownership and direction. The framework expects Phase A and Phase B
handover in some identity-specific form. It does **not** require every
identity to visit the four regions in one universal local order, at one
universal speed, cadence, proportion or observable sampling interval.

Di-ARA is the foundation on which the movement is expressed, not a claim that
all identities have the same gait.

## Correct reading of T342

T342 established the following descriptive facts under its frozen coordinate:

- all seven holdouts populated all four mixed regions;
- different domains retained different aspects of chronological structure;
- the qutrit alone passed both auxiliary common-gait endpoints;
- exact `e` and reciprocal-Phi were not supported as universal pure-axis
  constants by the registered strong gates.

Four-region occupancy is compatible with the intended Di-ARA geometry, but it
is not by itself a proof because the coordinate defines those four possible
regions. The registered `1/6` result rejects only the added universal-gait
hypothesis. It does not test whether an intact pair forms a stronger,
information-bearing parent relation than its separate or deliberately broken
components.

Therefore the status boundary is:

- **registered universal-gait auxiliary:** **NOT SUPPORTED**;
- **intended intact-pair Di-ARA coupling claim:** **NOT TESTED BY T342**;
- **four-region descriptive geometry:** **observed in 7/7 holdouts**;
- **universal `e`/Phi endpoints:** **NOT SUPPORTED BY T342**.

## Required successor

The direct successor must compare each intact declared pair with child-only
and broken-pair controls. It must allow each identity to learn its own
transition structure. Its question is whether coupling the two ARA axes adds
stable, transferable information at the parent level—not whether every
domain walks the quadrants in the same order.

## Provenance rule

The frozen T342 protocol and computational addendum remain byte-for-byte
unchanged. This dated clarification must travel with the report so future
readers and AI sessions cannot mistake the auxiliary universal-gait failure
for a failure of the originator's intended Di-ARA claim.
