# T363 claim packet — fault-tension Irrationality Di-ARA

**Frozen:** 12 August 2026, before calculating the tension coordinates  
**Evidence class:** public physical-system test with one dense local event and 15 repeated laboratory stress drops  
**Primary source:** Zhuo et al., Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.12177320  
**Replication source:** Acosta et al., Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.3979326

## Correction from T362

T362 tested local stress plus displacement magnitude as a movement navigator. It did not test the fault's tension identity. T363 is a separate frozen test and does not reinterpret or rescue T362.

## Claim under test

For the declared local fault identity, stored shear tension and signed tension transfer should form a physical Di-ARA. The ordered path should record accumulation, release at slip, and the beginning of reconnection/reloading. Its higher Irrationality parent should respond to that physical handover more specifically than order-destroyed and single-coordinate controls.

Displacement is not a tension coordinate. It supplies an independent physical marker for the main slip against which the tension-only path is scored.

## Required evidence

1. The tension axes must be calculated from stress storage and signed stress transfer, not displacement magnitude.
2. The dense tension path must retain more than one mixture state and show the declared accumulation → release → renewed-accumulation order around independently marked slip.
3. The higher `(x_P,x_R)` history parent must make its strongest transition close to slip and traverse more than one parent quadrant.
4. Correct chronology must locate that transition more specifically than value-preserving time shuffles, reversed order, storage alone, or removal of the transfer sign.
5. The same child-level tension handover must recur across the 10 dry and 5 fluid-pressure stress-drop events.
6. The higher Irrationality-parent handover must also recur across those events; otherwise only the ordinary tension ARA, not its proposed Irrationality parent, is supported.

## Evidence boundary

Passing supports this stress-storage/transfer realization on these laboratory faults. It does not establish field-earthquake prediction, a universal irrational constant, universal ARA generation, or uniqueness of the chosen smoothing and history estimators.
