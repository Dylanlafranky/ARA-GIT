# T351 frozen claim packet v1 — progressive zipper transfer

**Frozen:** 11 August 2026, before implementation or scoring  
**Evidence class:** synthetic known-referee causal instrument calibration

## ARA claim under test

An open parent traversal need not already possess a completed parent-level
Connection. As its current handover approaches, compatible lower-rung children
may begin to form local locks. Ordered local locks remain behind the moving
front and coarse-grain into a retained history/Connection parent.

In shorthand:

`open traversal -> candidate lower-rung meeting -> local information lock -> retained ordered Connection`.

The current tick is therefore proposed as the location of the active seam, not
as a container holding the complete history behind it.

## Necessary measurement distinction

Geometric approach is not itself declared to be Connection.

- **Candidate geometry:** causal ARA phase proximity and approach direction of
  paired lower-rung children.
- **Observed Connection:** shared response to an independent perturbation,
  measured causally and without reading the hidden edge state.
- **Retained history:** the ordered sequence of independently detected local
  locks.

If candidate geometry is identical but the independent shared response is
absent, the instrument must not claim that Connection has been established.

## Frozen alternatives

1. **Progressive zipper:** lower-rung connections form progressively behind the
   moving seam before parent closure.
2. **Memory-only mimic:** phase paths are identical to the progressive zipper,
   but no connections form. Geometry alone must be unable to distinguish it.
3. **Late snap:** candidate geometry develops progressively, but connections
   appear only at the final parent boundary.
4. **False seam:** nearby children have the wrong partner identity; proximity
   does not create stable ordered locks.
5. **Reverse unzip:** established locks release in the reverse of their forward
   locking order when the seam reverses.
6. **Interrupted zip:** the parent front pauses while compatible children at the
   seam remain in contact. Progressive lower-rung locking may continue during
   the pause even though parent-front motion is approximately zero.

## Frozen predictions

- In progressive-zip events, independently measured Connection rises before
  final parent closure and remains behind the seam.
- Candidate-geometry change precedes or coincides with lower-rung Connection;
  Connection does not lead candidate compatibility.
- Detected lock order follows the known child order.
- During an interrupted zip, Connection continues to build locally while the
  parent front is stationary.
- During reverse unzip, independently detected unlocks occur in reverse order.
- Geometry-only scoring remains at chance between the progressive zipper and
  its exact memory-only phase-path mimic.
- Adding the independent lower-rung response distinguishes those two regimes.

## Interpretation boundary

Passing calibrates a measurable progressive-zip signature and shows what data
would be required to test it in a real system. It does not establish that every
physical history is made by this mechanism, nor that geometric proximity alone
proves a hidden Connection.
