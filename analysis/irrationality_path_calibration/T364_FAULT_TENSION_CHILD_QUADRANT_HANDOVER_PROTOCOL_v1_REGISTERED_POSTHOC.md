## Disclosed observations before registration

T363 already showed that stored tension and release become nearly equal close to
slip. Exploratory inspection also showed that the dense equality occurs inside
the parent `Ab` quadrant and that the 15 extracted stress-drop records contain
near-event equality. T364 is therefore **not an untouched confirmation** of the
existence or timing of this pattern. It formalises the correct ARA scale,
quantifies controls and separates the local handover from the broader
Irrationality-history response.

## WHO

- `x_S`: low → high stored shear tension on a 0–2 ARA diameter.
- `x_F`: accumulation-dominant → release-dominant signed stress transfer on a
  second 0–2 ARA diameter.
- The active child inside parent quadrant `Ab`, where `x_S>=1` and `x_F>=1`.
- The existing higher path/history coordinates `x_P`, `x_R` and closure

## WHAT

Quadrant occupancy is an **address**, not a completeness gate. An identity is
not required to traverse all four parent quadrants. Inside `Ab`, decompress the
active child:

\[
u=2(x_S-1),\qquad v=2(x_F-1),\qquad u,v\in[0,2].
\]

Its relational ARA coordinate is

\[
h_{Ab}=\frac{2v}{u+v}.
\]

Therefore:

- `h_Ab<1`: stored-tension child leads;
- `h_Ab=1`: equal child handover, equivalent to `x_S=x_F`;
- `h_Ab>1`: release child leads.

The local child-ridge crossing is the first causal transition
`h_Ab<1 -> h_Ab>=1` while consecutive readings remain inside `Ab`.

The broader Irrationality-history response remains separate. `x_R>1` denotes
the open/stochastic-leaning side of that already defined path-history cut;
`x_R<1` denotes its determinate/reused-leaning side. T364 asks whether release
occurs at a local child ridge while the broader history opens and later
recloses. It does not force both scales to cross their ridges simultaneously.

1. Preserve all T363 coordinates and physical event markers unchanged.

6. Separately record the broader history side at release, its first opening
