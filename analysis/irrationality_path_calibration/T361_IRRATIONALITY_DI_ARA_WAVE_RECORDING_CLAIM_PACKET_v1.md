# T361 claim packet — Irrationality Di-ARA wave recording and recovery

**Frozen:** 12 August 2026, before T361 outcome scoring  
**Evidence class:** controlled public physical-system instrument test  
**Source:** Ocampo-Espindola et al., Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15122129

## Claim under test

The Irrationality Di-ARA is being tested as a relational wave recorder. Given an earlier chronological record of two coupled raw physical waves, its retained two-axis path, four direction states and local movement relations should contain enough information to reconstruct later child-wave cycles from the continuing visible parent wave and the child's entry state.

This is not a chance-classification claim. The primary question is whether the instrument records the wave relation it claims to measure.

## Required evidence

1. Raw current amplitude and within-cycle waveform shape must be retained. Neither oscillator may be converted into a linear phase sawtooth.
2. The measured Di-ARA path must expose both 0–2 identity coordinates and all four direction states.
3. An untouched chronological suffix must be reconstructed from a recorder built only on the earlier prefix.
4. Reconstruction must be scored by waveform shape, direction, quadrant traversal, turning points and return/closure—not merely a label or hit rate.
5. Coarse-graining the recovered children must reconstruct the population-level child parent.
6. Direction-blind and wrong-lineage recorders must determine whether success belongs to the four-state relation and to the measured coupling.

## Evidence boundary

Passing would support this specific Irrationality Di-ARA recorder on one controlled physical archive. It would not establish universal bedrock geometry, prove exact mathematical irrationality, or show that every hidden physical wave is recoverable from one visible partner.
