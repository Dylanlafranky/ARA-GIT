## Claim under test

The frozen T348/T357 path-history instrument can distinguish three physical relations without using their published synchronization labels inside the coordinates:

1. a phase-locked coupled pair;
2. a coherently and repeatedly non-closing coupled pair;
3. uncoupled frequency drift.

The specific prediction carried forward from T357 is that deliberate detuning can produce an ordered nonzero child-phase miss at the one-parent-cycle return even when exact closure is absent. Coupling must add history or closure structure beyond that obtained from two uncoupled clocks with different intrinsic frequencies.

## Frozen ARA translation

- One oscillator in population 1 supplies the parent clock.
- The correspondingly indexed oscillator in population 2 supplies the child path.
- Eight equal parent-phase landmarks define one parent cycle.
- `x_P` measures whether child addresses are reused or continue opening.
- `x_R` measures how much movement remains unexplained by ordered local history.
- `C(H)` keeps return coherence and signed miss separate.
- A coherent miss requires high return coherence and a nonzero miss; an arbitrary wandering path does not qualify.
- Pure poles are reference limits. Every experimental identity may sit on a gradient.

## Confirmation pattern

The strongest result requires all of the following:

1. the declared locked reference closes at one parent cycle;
2. at least part of the detuning sweep has a coherent, nonzero one-cycle miss while retaining low `x_R`;
3. time shuffling damages history information while preserving address support;
4. uncoupled detuned controls are less relation-specific than the true coupled paths;
5. deliberately mismatching records damages prediction or coherent closure;
6. reversal preserves unsigned geometry and reverses orientation.

## Falsifiers

- No coupled detuning record shows coherent non-closure.
- Candidate records are as incoherent as shuffled paths.
- Uncoupled detuned pairs perform as well as coupled pairs on all relation-sensitive measures.
- The declared locked reference does not close.
- Results depend on one electrode pair rather than the record-level distribution.

## Forbidden rescue

No Fourier transform, Hilbert transform, fitted frequency, published synchronization label, Phi, `e`, `1/e`, rational approximation, or post-hoc phase remapping may enter the primary coordinates or rescue a failed gate.

## Boundary

Finite recordings can support structured non-closure over an observed horizon. They cannot prove that a real-valued frequency ratio is number-theoretically irrational or establish a universal ARA law.
