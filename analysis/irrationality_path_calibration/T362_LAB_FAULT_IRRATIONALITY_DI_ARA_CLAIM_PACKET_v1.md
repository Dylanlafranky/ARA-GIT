# T362 claim packet - laboratory-fault Irrationality Di-ARA

**Frozen:** 12 August 2026, before T362 coordinate scoring  
**Evidence class:** public physical-system test with a dense primary rupture and repeated-cycle replication  
**Primary source:** Zhuo et al., Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.12177320  
**Replication source:** Acosta et al., Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.3979326

## Claim under test

The Irrationality Di-ARA should record a real stick-slip handover when connection and movement are measured independently. In a laboratory fault, a local shear-stress cut and a nearby local fault-displacement cut should form a chronological two-axis path whose higher parent reading changes at the physical loading-to-slip interface. The path-history reading should contain more useful movement information than either axis alone, a direction-blind cut, a wrong stress-slip pairing, or destroyed chronology.

This is not a claim that earthquakes are predictable from this one experiment. It is a direct test of whether the proposed Irrationality Di-ARA records the physical relation it is said to measure.

## Required evidence

1. Connection and movement must come from independent, synchronized sensors rather than an algebraic complement.
2. The raw physical Di-ARA must occupy more than one trivial state and retain the loading, slip and arrest geometry.
3. The sliding Irrationality parent must occupy at least two of its four gradient sectors and register its strongest handover close to the independently measured main slip.
4. Chronological pairing must locate the handover more faithfully than broken-time, wrong-pair and single-axis controls.
5. On an untouched chronological suffix, the full two-axis directional relation must improve immediate movement recovery over the declared broken-geometry controls.
6. The connection-side handover must recur in the independent 10 dry and 5 fluid-pressure laboratory cycles.

## Evidence boundary

Passing would support this specific physical realization of Irrationality Di-ARA across one dense rupture and a small repeated-cycle replication archive. It would not prove tectonic-earthquake prediction, universal fractal-sphere generation, unique ARA coordinates, or a universal irrational constant.
