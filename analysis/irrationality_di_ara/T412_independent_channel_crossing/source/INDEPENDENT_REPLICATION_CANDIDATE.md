# Independent replication candidate — not yet scored

The files in this directory come from the public *Bursting of condensates* dataset (Zenodo record 8337749). They contain 20 measured aspiration-to-burst transitions across three protein identities with both pre-burst aspiration trajectories and post-burst radius measurements.

They were downloaded while preparing T412 as a possible independent movement-heavy replication. They were **not** used in the frozen T412 result because the exact zero-crossing rule failed its timing-concentration gate in the original filament data. Carrying a failed landmark into a new physical identity would obscure rather than confirm the result.

Before using this dataset, freeze an identity-faithful parent/child/grandchild scale construction for the aspiration signal and predeclare whether the retained continuous balance is tested by level, slope, or ridge-normalized combination.
