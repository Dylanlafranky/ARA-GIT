## Strongest diagnosis

The failure is narrower than “spacetime does not follow ARA” and stronger than
“nothing happened.” The frozen **detector-level operationalisation** did not
expose the previously observed hourglass route.

The visuals show why:

1. most paths remain in the low-connection half of the plane;
2. movement appears as isolated ridge jumps rather than a persistent branch;
3. three events are too weak for the two-detector raw activity gate;
4. wrong-event pairing has higher median coordinate agreement (`0.880`) than
   the same-event stage reading (`0.700`) because all low-information tracks
   share the same lower basin. That “agreement” is background compression,
   not a recovered physical relation.

The likely methodological problem is therefore not an ARA landmark that needs
to be moved after seeing the answer. It is that local spectral concentration
is not an adequate raw-strain Connection observable at these detector signal
levels, while ridge displacement is too noise-sensitive to recover continuous
movement without a stronger source or additional independent relation.
