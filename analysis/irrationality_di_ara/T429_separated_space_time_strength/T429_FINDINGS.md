# T429 findings — separated Space/Time strain and gravitational strength

## Frozen verdict

The primary separated-cut claim was **not supported**.

- positive time/movement maturity trend: 0/5 holdouts;
- positive space/connection maturity trend: 0/5;
- both late histories above the 90th off-source percentile: 0/5;
- positive `S_B` versus binding-proxy association: 1/5;

No gate or event identity was changed after scoring.

## What was actually separated

The model-free side was constructed before source parameters were loaded:

- `T_A` used spectral centroid frequency and positive chirp-rate only;
- `S_B` used received spectral amount and independent H1/L1 amount agreement;
- all component features were projected independently to 0–2 from each
  detector's off-source distribution;
- no complement, sum-to-two relation, event ordering or merger template was
  forced.

The established-physics crosswalk was calculated afterward from official GWOSC
component masses, redshift and distance plus the already measured consensus
frequency. It supplied separation, compactness, binding and inspiral-time
proxies, but could not alter the ARA histories.

## What the result says

Separating frequency/phase from amount/agreement was methodologically correct,
but the broad 1.25-second, model-free projection did not isolate the weak source
wave from detector noise. The paths occupy structured parts of the 0–2 plane,
but their chronological ordering is not stable across events and their late
values are not unusually high relative to off-source windows.

This is therefore not evidence for the requested opening/closing Irrationality
Di-ARA handover. It is a useful failure: the earlier apparent two-coordinate
shape cannot be rescued merely by relabelling one axis Space and the other Time.

## Gravitational-strength boundary

Two different quantities are now recorded separately:

1. **Received strength:** calibrated detector strain and model-free spectral
   amount. Distance, orientation, antenna response and detector noise affect it.
2. **Source coupling:** an established-physics proxy inferred from catalog
   masses/redshift and measured frequency. It is not directly recorded by one
   detector and is not an ARA coordinate.
